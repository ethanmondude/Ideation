Clean Feed v1

The real-or-copycat checker for Solana addresses, tickers and links in your X feed

24 Oct 2026, 10:21am AWST 7/10

Executive summary

  • Lead job. On every Solana contract address (CA), $ticker or token link in the X feed, replies and Communities, Clean Feed automatically stamps one of four verdicts, with no paste and no click:
  • real $X;
  • copy #7 of 9 of $X → real: 25X5…pump;
  • no confirmed real mint;
  • new, unverified.

It also collapses the shill and phishing replies around that CA with a one-line reason ("same text as 14 other replies in 3 min"). General scam and fluff filtering supports the lead but isn't the lead. - Problem. - Copycat mints arrive by the dozen. Trench Bot logged 215 brand name-bait mints among 314 new $500k coins in about 25 hours (SpaceX 17, IShowSpeed 11, YouTube 11, Gemini 11, MrBeast 10). - Caller rings and "airdrop page / 10% supply / allocation scan" phishing ride every hot coin. In X bot's 119 shill checks, 44 of the 48 shilled or mixed coins carried a pattern the extension could collapse in the page (this run's free test). - Traders can't tell which CA in a reply is the real one. - Headline feature: the automatic copycat-mint stamp. The collapse bar only supports it. BotBlind (free, launched 21 Oct) already ships a reason bar with Show and Not-a-bot buttons and 3 strictness modes, so the bar alone caps uniqueness at 4 (Project Bot). - Rivals to beat: Raindrop, fable and BotBlind. - fable is free and open source on x.com only. It puts a scam/caution/trusted line under crypto posts, adds SCAM/RUG/SHILL stamps, CA and $ticker underlines, and a profile backstory panel. It has 57 Chrome users, is Robinhood-Chain-first, pays $FABLE stakers from revenue, and uploads scrolled posts by default. - Raindrop already detects every CA, pump.fun mint and ticker in the X timeline, with price, RugCheck data and one-tap buy. - PastTokenBot already lists older same-ticker tokens, but only when you paste a CA on its website. - BotBlind collapses spam, scam and AI-bot replies, but by its own description it has no contract or crypto-template checks (untested). - Nobody stamps "real or copy #N of $X" automatically on every CA in the feed. - Who pays and the token's job. Desktop memecoin and perps traders. The core is free. Pro is US$9/month or held $CFEED, and unlocks unlimited automatic verdicts, Communities safe mode and copycat alerts on coins you hold. Agents pay x402 at US$0.005 per check. Token = hold to unlock access, and burn your own for deep checks and credits. That's all. No holder payouts, no revenue buybacks, and no paying anyone from slashed stakes. - Cost against A$1,000. About A$72 to day 30 and A$545 over 4 months (estimates), including the A$300 legal consult and the A$83 launch. Server, RPC and LLM spend has a hard cap of A$25 a month; above it the extension falls back to local-only folding. RobinSight, a token-backed X scanner, shut down because of server costs. - Main risks. - fable and Raindrop are one release away from a copycat badge, and BotBlind could add crypto rules. - Nobody has been shown paying a subscription for an X scam filter. Security extensions earn from swap fees (Scam Sniffer 0.25%; Pocket Universe and Kerberus 0.8-0.95%), and that's execution, which our rules avoid. - X's ToS (effective 9 Oct 2026) bans scraping. - X DOM changes will break things. - There's no mobile app support. - A single wrong "real" call that drains someone would sink trust. - Proof plan. v1 only counts as beating fable if it passes all three blind sets: Mint Seal's frozen holdout, Trench Bot's 182 held-back cases, and Project Bot's 60-post feed set. The pass lines are below. The hard cases get their own line in the report. - Verdict: test, about 30%. The lead is the right shape for tech bros: instant, automatic, inside X, read-only. But it's a narrow edge with three fast rivals next to it, and no payer shown yet. Gate c (crowded) and gate e (money) are shaky.

Innovation, usability, uniqueness

  • Demand you can see (X bot, public posts):
  • @alexrkonrad (91k followers) and @AdamLowisz (55k) complain about the scam and spam layer on X.
  • @kimiloo: "drainer links under literally every tweet".
  • X bot's demand.jsonl has 20 posts: 16 trader complaints, workarounds, wishes or builders, and 3 projects warning "beware of fake tokens".
  • This is demand for relief, not proof that anyone pays.
  • Innovation: 6/10. Why it's possible now:
  • Same-ticker copies now arrive by the dozen within hours (Trench Bot run 10).
  • pumpdata.fun gives free deployer history and launch-buyer counts with no key.
  • Cheap Solana RPC reads give mint age, authorities, holders and pool reserves in about a second.
  • Caller rings reuse the same accounts across coins: 25 of 26 named ring accounts recur in X bot's records.

A year ago you couldn't build an automatic, per-CA "real or copy #N" answer inside the feed: the copycat volume and the free per-mint data weren't there. The collapse UI isn't new (BotBlind, fable). - Usability: 8/10 (Git bot's score; first use under 30 seconds inside X): 1. Click "Add to Chrome". 2. Open x.com. No sign-up, wallet or X login needed. 3. Every CA in view already shows its stamp, e.g. "copy #3 of 5 of $AOP · real: 25X5…pump · deployer 41 launches, 0 graduated". Click it for the "why" panel. 4. Open that post's replies. The shill and phishing replies around the CA are already collapsed, e.g. "fake airdrop link · same text as 9 others". 5. Flip filtered/raw, or drag the strictness slider.

Minus: mobile isn't supported, since there are no extensions in the X app. - Uniqueness: 5/10. Problem and user

  • User: tech-bro traders and builders who find coins on CT and in X Communities on desktop Chrome, Brave or Arc, next to Axiom, GMGN or Photon. Also caller-group admins and Community mods.
  • Design data (Trench Bot, 398 design cases; results on it are method checks only):
  • Fake CAs: 87, including 15 where the real mint is known (Kimchi, AOP, PHP families).
  • Brand-name coins: 210. These are easy, with many near-duplicates (17 SpaceX mints).
  • Phishing accounts: 13 ("airdrop page" and "10% supply / check MemePage" templates).
  • Shill-ring accounts: 59, plus 9 paid-attention coins.
  • Non-scam cases: 20 (7 coins, 13 accounts).
  • Gaps: no confirmed drainer URLs (t.co links only), no Communities data, labels on 51 shill accounts are low confidence, and 20 non-scams against roughly 370 scams can't show false-flag rates.
  • Hard cases:
  • The "airdrop page" and "10% supply" spam also appears on coins that held up (Kimchi, CHOO, ヤギ, WRHND). Clean Feed must collapse the spam reply and leave the coin's stamp alone.
  • Scam-warning accounts (@RaOnChain, @allenson5002, @StiffMem) must stay visible.
  • A large KOL is labelled as paid promo. That label is private only.
  • Fake US fund-name coin farms (launched 21 Oct; Trench Bot run 11) show $2-282M caps with no wallet above about 0.6%, so they pass holder and concentration tests. Only an unpaid-poster / paid-attention check catches them.
  • Risky survivors with all-paid attention, e.g. $shiit (412ECWXN3jGbd1kn8ck7eeupWttkmrFkNm9JqfAzpump): every X mention is paid callers and reply spam. The stamp should say "risky · attention is paid callers only", not "fake".
  • X bot's genuine hard cases:
    • an organic $KIMCHI "airdrop" post;
    • a single reply carrying the real Kimchi CA;
    • Phantom's "Refer friends, get paid";
    • pump.fun's $2.5M competition post;
    • Solana's "BREAKING: $AAPL is now live";
    • Cointelegraph's Netanyahu story, posted while $NETANYAHU coins were being pushed.
  • Patterns from X bot (public search):
  • AOP: the AlphaPulsevw, clebers1984 and Davide_Alp5y2 accounts posted identical "$61K → $56.4M 924X" text.
  • $ODIE: one account posted 15+ identical CA replies in 3 minutes.
  • 1SHARE: about 12 aged accounts posted a paired "printing massively on my Alpha telegram" + "164.07x" template.
  • WRHND and YMB: 0-follower accounts posted "10% of the supply is being distributed to holders… check your eligibility on MemePage" (example).
  • Other templates: "allocation scan"; "Next 100x gem loading"; "913x"; "CA=" airdrop text.
  • Farmed hype: $FRENS paid floods, AgentDojo's identical paragraphs.
  • A copycat example from Trench Bot: posts about "Kimchi rugged" were about Project Kimchi (B1Y2…pump), not the main Kimchi.
  • Extension trust is low. Crypto Copilot, an "X swap" extension, secretly skimmed Solana swaps. So: read-only, no wallet permissions, open source.
  • Today's workaround: paste the CA into PastTokenBot, RugCheck or a terminal; tag @Larpcheckbot or @grok; mute words by hand. Wallets catch drainers only at signing.

Product and UX

MVP wedge (2-3 weeks solo; Git bot's estimate): automatic copycat stamp + shill and phishing reply collapse.

  1. Copycat stamp (Mint Seal inside). - Detects Solana mints (base58, …pump), $tickers and DexScreener, pump.fun or Axiom links in the feed, replies and Communities. - Lists same-ticker and same-name siblings using pump.fun and DexScreener search, for the candidate list only. - Decides the verdict from on-chain reads plus deployer history:
    • mint creation slot and age;
    • mint and freeze authority;
    • holder count;
    • pool reserves read from the pool account (never DexScreener liquidity, which copycats fake into the billions);
    • number of distinct launch buyers and deployer launch record (pumpdata.fun), not bundle share, since a 79.31% curve buyout is now normal;
    • the CA pinned by the project's own X account, if any;
    • Jupiter's verified tag.
    • Attention signal: what share of a mint's X posters are known template or caller accounts versus unpaid organic posters. It's computed locally from the posts in view plus the private ring memory, and adds a risky · paid attention only modifier. It's needed because fund-name farms pass every holder test.
    • Never shows green without two independent signals agreeing. Otherwise it shows "no confirmed real mint".
    • Handles migrations (SLERF, MYRO: "moved to new mint"). No hardcoded tickers.
    • Labels are text in our own style, never X-like checkmarks.
  2. Reply collapse around the CA. - In-page duplicate detection: N+ accounts posting the same text within M minutes, one account posting the same CA reply 15+ times. - Known templates (gain-multiple, "Alpha telegram", "airdrop page", "10% supply / check eligibility", "allocation scan"). - Link domains checked against MetaMask eth-phishing-detect, scamsniffer/scam-database and phantom/blocklist (licences to confirm), plus lookalike-domain rules. - Reply-level, not coin-level: collapsing spam under Kimchi doesn't change Kimchi's stamp. - Scam-warning replies ("this is a copycat, real CA is…") are kept and boosted, not hidden.
  3. UX. - Collapse, never delete. One-line reason. "Why" panel. Filtered/raw toggle. - Strictness slider. Per-account always-show and always-hide, stored locally. - Communities safe mode (strict preset).

Later, after the money test:

  • Tapeline "first reported Xh ago" stamp, off by default until v4 retrieval passes.
  • Decoy link sandbox.
  • Private paid-promo labels (never public).
  • Telegram copycat alerts.
  • Firefox.

Stack and data flow (designed for X's ToS).

  • Chrome MV3, host permission x.com only. No remotely fetched code: rules ship as data only.
  • Posts are checked locally. Only the CA, link domain or handle being checked is sent to our API. Never post text, never bulk uploads, no default "help improve" upload (unlike fable).
  • API on a A$10/month VPS (Hono) with a Redis cache per mint, free-tier Solana RPC and pumpdata.fun free.
  • Hard spend cap of A$25/month across server, RPC and LLM. Checks only run when a CA scrolls into view, and are cached per mint across all users. At the cap, new uncached mints show "unverified" and folding stays local.
  • Payments: Lemon Squeezy card, Solana Pay USDC, optional $CFEED hold check by wallet signature (sign-in only, no transaction permissions).

Week by week (20-25 h/week).

  1. DOM layer, template and duplicate rules, reason line, raw toggle. Method-check on the 398 design cases, reported as method only.
  2. Copycat engine: candidate lookup, on-chain reads, verdict rules, cache, cost cap.
  3. Link checks, slider, Communities preset, Pro gating, MCP + x402.
  4. Unlisted CWS listing. Hand the build to the three blind runs.

What's hard.

  • X DOM churn.
  • Telling a genuinely excited reply from a template.
  • Finding "the real one" when the creator never pins a CA (the Kimchi dispute).
  • Cross-chain impostors (EVM copies of Solana coins).

Token design (pump.fun)

  • Only two jobs:
  • (1) Hold $CFEED to unlock Pro: unlimited automatic stamps, Communities safe mode, copycat alerts. Checked by wallet signature. Card users never need the token.
  • (2) Burn your own for deep checks (deployer funding graph, cross-chain impostors) and for API/x402 credits.
  • Disputes: a user can lock their own $CFEED to dispute a verdict. If upheld, they get it back and the verdict changes. If frivolous, their own stake is burned. Nobody is ever paid from slashed stakes or fees. Git bot's "stake-to-report" idea is cut back to this, because paying correct reporters would be a holder payout.
  • No holder payouts, no revenue buybacks, no creator-fee buy-and-burn. Card and USDC revenue is never used to buy the token. Creator fees go to a published wallet as a bonus only.
  • Launch: pump.fun, after the first 25 paying users and the legal consult. Mint and freeze authority off, confirmed on-chain. Small labelled dev buy (≤0.5 SOL, wallet published). No bundles, no paid callers. Pin one official CA on the site, on the X account and inside the extension from day one.
  • Dogfood launch: the extension stamps every $CFEED copycat as "copy #N of $CFEED". Copycats show up within hours (about 10 for Kimchi), so launch day doubles as the live demo.
  • Optional 2-week hook: pump.fun's US$2.5M PnL competition (sign-up by 26 Oct, runs to 9 Nov). Any public post needs Ethan's OK.
  • Comparables we must not copy: fable ($FABLE staking from revenue), Raindrop (50% swap-fee share to $RAIN), Trenchee (fee rewards to holders). Comparable we can copy: Larpcheck (hold-to-access).
  • Token fit: about 6/10 (Git bot also scored about 6).
  • Legal line: usage credit only. Labels stay factual ("newer mint, different deployer, 0 graduations") and never accuse named people. A$300 consult. Not legal advice.

Platform and policy risk

  • X ToS (effective 9 Oct 2026) bans access "by any means… other than through our currently available, published interfaces" and "crawling or scraping… in any form" without consent (x.com/en/tos via Project Bot; TechCrunch on the 2023 change).
  • Mitigation: checks run locally on what the user's browser has already loaded. Only the CA, link or handle being checked goes to the server. Nothing is stored, nothing is auto-blocked or auto-posted, and there are no public account scorecards or shared handle blocklists until legal review.
  • Remaining risk is grey, not zero. Control Panel for Twitter (300k users) shows long-lived X DOM rewriting can survive.
  • Chrome Web Store:
  • One narrow purpose ("verify crypto content on X"), with no swaps or trading bolted on.
  • Limited Use disclosure (policy).
  • No remote code.
  • Badges must not imitate X's UI or verified marks.
  • Token promotion inside the extension invites review scrutiny, so keep the hold check on the website.
  • DOM breakage: a selector health check plus fast patch releases. Founder time is budgeted for it.
  • Liability: we can't offer Pocket-Universe-style US$30k coverage. Default to "unverified". Calling a copycat "real" is the one error with a zero-tolerance pass line.

1. Summary and one-minute wow

Marshal is a read-only Chrome MV3 extension (Brave, Arc and Edge work; Firefox later) for desktop Solana traders who find coins on X and buy on GMGN, Jupiter, pump.fun, Axiom or Photon. On x.com it folds drainer, airdrop-lure and copy-paste shill replies into a grey bar with a reason, puts a factual pill on every CA, $ticker and token link, flags a reply CA that differs from the thread author's, shows "claimed $X · live $Y" when a post brags about market cap, and marks coins pushed by a shill ring it has seen before. When you click through to a coin page it remembers the post; if the page's mint is different, the toolbar icon turns red and a notification says "Not the mint in the post you came from (@author, 1 min ago)". Opt-in: paste a public wallet address and, on your device only, each account that sent you to a coin gets a small stamp with your own result ("led you here 3× · −1.4 SOL · 1 of 3 held 24h"). No wallet connect, no signing, no buy button, no sign-up, no LLM; post text, handles and wallets never leave the browser.

One-minute wow: install, open a memecoin reply thread: lure replies are folded, one reply's CA has a red pill ("different CA from the one @author posted"), a milestone post shows "claimed $10.6M · live $427k". Click a reply's chart link to GMGN: the Marshal icon goes red and a notification says it isn't the mint from the post you came from. Nothing to set up.

2. Target user

Tech-bro desktop traders and builders (Chrome, Brave, Arc) who trade off crypto Twitter and X Communities. They pay by card, USDC, or by holding $MRSHL after launch. Mobile is out (the X app takes no extensions).

8. Core logic

Guard (gated, off-page):

on x.com click(a) where a.href host in TERMINALS or post has a CA:
   m = mint_from_url(a.href) or post.raw_ca or link_text_ca          # never fetch t.co
   session.clicks.push({m, postId, author, ts: now}); idb.clicks.put(same)   # idb kept 90 days
terminal.content.ts (read-only): send {path: location.pathname, referrer: document.referrer, first_load} to background
background: p = resolve(path); c = newest click
   alarm only if c && now - c.ts <= 120 s && first route load since c
       && (referrer is t.co or x.com, or referrer == "") && p != c.m
   -> action.setBadgeText("!") red; notifications.create("Not the mint in the post you came from (@{c.author}, {age})")
   never write to the terminal DOM, read cookies/storage, touch the wallet or call terminal APIs

Led-you-here (on device):

opt-in: wallets[] = pasted base58 pubkeys (≤3); start = now; rpc = user_key ? helius : public
after each recorded click c on mint m: chrome.alarms at +2, +10, +30 min
poll(w): sigs = getSignaturesForAddress(w, {until: w.last_sig, limit: 25})
   for s in sigs: tx = getTransaction(s, jsonParsed)
      d = post − pre token balance where owner == w and mint == m          # owner, never signer
      if d > 0 and 0 <= tx.blockTime − c.ts <= 1800 s:
         spent = SOL out of w (minus fees), or USDC out → USD; any other quote → entry "unknown"
         attribute to the most recent click on m before blockTime; other clicks on m → "also seen from"
outcomes (cached; re-check +1 h, +24 h, then daily while held; ≤10 GeckoTerminal calls/min):
   crash_1h = min 5-min low in [t, t+1h] <= 0.2 × entry_usd
   held_24h = price(t+24h) >= 0.5 × entry_usd
   sol_result = SOL in from w's sells of m − spent (+ held tokens × DexScreener price, labelled "marked")
stamp(author) only with ≥1 matched buy; never upload, never a 0–10 score, never green, never on 0-buy accounts
backfill: same loop from install day only (no clicks exist earlier), with ETA and cancel

Rings: gain-line posts; 3+ accounts on one CA within 10 min = an edge; ring = 3+ accounts each with edges on 2+ CAs; join only on repeated links; 14-day decay. Relay pair: post q by b ≈ post p by a (normalised-text Jaccard ≥0.8 or the same number sequence), 0 < t_q − t_p ≤ 30 min, same CA → one source for counts, edge(a,b). Linked to ring: ≥2 shared windows with members of ring R on ≥1 CA but fails the 2-CA rule → grey "linked to ring R (1 coin)", never member, never red, 14-day decay. R11: post_ms = (id >> 22) + 1288834974657; amber "linked post is {d} days older than this coin and doesn't name it" if post_ms < mint_created_ms and the post text names neither ticker nor CA. R12 (server): one wallet (not the pool) holds ≥90% of supply, or two hold ≥49% each → red "one wallet holds N% of supply" (Trench Bot clean-feed-build-rules.md). Quote token: read the pool's quote mint first; price non-SOL quotes via DexScreener/Jupiter; show "liquidity unknown", never $0.
