Clean Feed v2

The X-native scam layer for crypto Twitter. Every CA in posts and replies gets a factual family stamp, scam replies fold with a crypto-specific reason, and "the real one is X" stays off until it's proven

24 Oct 2026, 12:30pm AWST 5.5/10 Status: Pitched v2, awaiting Project Bot re score vs v1 5.5 (re scored 5.0 at 10:46)

Executive summary

  • Problem: crypto Twitter is flooded with copycat mints and scam replies. Trench Bot logged 316 name-bait coins, all rugs, at about 8.8 an hour, and ~10 Kimchi-named copies around one real coin. "Drainer links under literally every tweet" (@kimiloo), and crypto reply bots fill "half the replies on any given post" (@alexrkonrad, 91k followers).
  • Product: a read-only Chrome extension for x.com. It automatically stamps every CA, $ticker and token link in posts and replies with a factual line ("1 of 12+ mints called $KIMCHI · oldest ≠ deepest · deployer 825 launches"). It flags hard rule hits in red: name-bait, fake fund names, the 13-14% dump template, a different CA from the one the author posted in this thread, and free-host drainer links. It folds scam replies with a one-line reason ("same CA in 11 other replies"). "The real one is X" ships off until it passes Mint Seal Part B.
  • Who pays and how much: desktop Solana memecoin traders. Free core (local folding plus 30 automatic stamps a day). Pro US$9/month or hold $CFEED (unlimited stamps, Communities strict mode, copy alerts on coins you hold). Agents pay x402 at US$0.005 per check (MCP free for 50 a day). No payer has been shown yet; every rival is free.
  • Token job: hold $CFEED for Pro, or burn your own for deep checks and API credits. No payouts, no buybacks, no revenue share. pump.fun launch only after ≥25 payers or ≥30% active installs, plus the legal consult. Mint and freeze authority off, a ≤0.5 SOL labelled dev buy, Holder Rewards and Tokenized Agent off, one pinned CA.
  • Cost vs A$1,000: about A$73 to day 30 (no token, no legal) and A$449-589 over 4 months including the A$300 legal consult (estimate) and the ~A$76 launch. That leaves ≥A$411 of headroom. Running cost is A$0/month on free tiers, with a hard A$25/month cap.
  • Path to US$10k/month: about 1,100 Pro subscribers, or ~730 Pro plus ~690k x402 checks a month (gross, before the 20-30% facilitator cut). Not the base case: base is US$1-3k MRR at month 12 (estimate).
  • Why now and edge: copy volume and free per-mint data are new (pumpdata.fun, Jupiter v2, a ~0.4s two-search family lookup measured this run). Rivals sit on trading pages (Buddy) or in Telegram (Vamp Alert, OGfinder, Rick). Nobody stamps every CA inside X threads and uses the thread itself as evidence.
  • Main risk, first move, verdict: the main risk is that Buddy or fable ships the same thing on x.com, and nobody pays for a free-able check. First move this week: Claude Code builds the local-only extension (week 1). In parallel, Claude Code collects 60 fresh posts unlabelled (Build task 0), and Ethan, or X bot if he asks, labels them in about 10 minutes before the rules switch on. Verdict: test, about 30% confident (unchanged from v1). The pivot removes v1's failed claim, but the new lead is still unproven blind and has no payer.

Product and UX

First minute (AT-23: under 30s, ≤5 steps, no sign-up/wallet/X login):

  1. Click "Add to Chrome".
  2. Open x.com. A one-line toast says: "Clean Feed is on · checks locally · only CAs leave your browser".
  3. Every CA, $ticker and token link already has a pill. Grey means no confirmed real mint · 1 of 12+ mints called $KIMCHI. Amber means a factual warning (oldest ≠ deepest, deployer 825 launches, 0 graduated). Red means a rule hit (name-bait: not made by SpaceX, different CA from the one @kimchi posted above, free-host page carrying a mint).
  4. Open any memecoin reply thread. Scam replies are already one grey bar: Hidden · 10%-supply airdrop lure · same text as 9 others · Show · Not spam.
  5. Click any pill for the "why" panel: signals for and against, the family list (headed by the copy count and a "not ranked" note; the post's own mint first, labelled "the one in this post", then the rest alphabetical by mint, never sorted by liquidity or launch time by default, with liquidity, holders and launch time as user-sortable columns), the deployer record, a copy-CA button and Solscan links. There's no buy button.

Popup: three controls only: Strictness (Relaxed / Balanced default / Strict), Raw view, Pro.

Advanced tech (what's new):

  • Thread context as evidence. The content script knows each post's conversation and author. It can say "this reply's CA differs from the CA the root author posted" and "this account dropped the same CA in 3+ conversations". It never has to know which mint is real.
  • Oldest ≠ deepest. Launch order (rivals' rule) and on-chain pool reserves read from the pool account are both shown. When they disagree, the stamp says so.
  • Paid-only attention: if a mint has ≥5 posts in the local 24h cache and 0 come from accounts that trip no shill rule, it gets · risky: attention is paid callers only.
  • Holder fingerprints from Trench Bot's log: the 13.0-14.6% / 0.24% / 0.05% dump template, 1 or 18-19 first-10s buyers at 79.31%, and round-lot supply parked in a famous wallet (a copy tell, never a real signal).
  • Local-only mode: text and link rules with zero network requests (AT-11).
  • Shadow-mode copy engine: it computes "real" answers and logs them for the Part B test without showing them.

What's hard:

  • X DOM churn (fail open, AT-25).
  • Templates that rotate wording. v1 caught 2/17 new ones before v2's rules.
  • Truncated family search. Both APIs cap results at 30 and 20 this run, so counts show as "12+".
  • Copy-first families.
  • Communities, where there is no test data yet.

What a copycat could clone:

  • Buddy or SafeCA could clone the CA pill in a release.
  • BotBlind or Raindrop could clone reply folding.
  • Harder to clone: thread-context rules tuned on X bot's and Trench Bot's labelled data, the weekly pattern refresh, and published blind accuracy.
