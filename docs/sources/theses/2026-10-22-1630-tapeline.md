# Tapeline: trade the AI headline, in one click, on Solana perps

*Idea bot thesis, Thu 22 Oct 2026, 16:30 AWST (Perth). Path: /workspace/idea-bot/theses/2026-10-22-1630-tapeline.md*

## Executive summary
- **Problem.** AI news now moves tradable markets around the clock: Anthropic and OpenAI pre-IPO perps, AI-linked stock perps (NVDA, IREN, CRWV) and AI coins. Tech-bro traders see the headline on X, then lose minutes working out which market it moves, whether the rumour is real, and which venue lists it. On 18 Oct an unconfirmed "Anthropic deal" post sent the IREN perp on Hyperliquid from about $26 to $44 and back ([NighthawkTradez](https://x.com/NighthawkTradez/status/2111862558416409005), 279k views; [Crosswatch](https://x.com/crosswatchhq/status/2111905714520395930)).
- **Product.** A browser overlay on X plus a Telegram bot. It detects an AI headline, shows the 1 to 5 markets it moves (for example ANTHROPIC, NVDA, IREN, SOL), adds a source check that labels the story confirmed, single-source or denied, and opens a one-tap long or short on **Phoenix perps** (Solana) through Phoenix's new **Flight builder codes**. Login is with a Solana wallet, and the trading key is a scoped session key in an embedded wallet that can't withdraw.
- **Who pays and how much.** Traders pay a builder fee of 5 basis points (0.05%) on filled taker volume, collected on-chain by Phoenix and public. This is the Hyperliquid builder-code model. There, 86 frontends earned **$3.80M in 30 days on $8.72B of routed volume**, and small specialist apps make $10k to $35k a month from a few hundred users (Mass: $35.1k from 258 users; Moonbot: $16.7k from 148) ([OpenChainBench, 22 Oct](https://openchainbench.com/hyperliquid)).
- **Token job.** $TAPE is staked to cut your builder fee from 5 to 2.5 bps and to unlock instant (zero-delay) alerts, and it's spent on deep source checks. It launches on Bags, so creator fees are visible on-chain. There are no buybacks from revenue and no payouts to holders.
- **Cost against A$1,000.** About A$560 one-off plus A$95 a month, so roughly A$940 covers four months of runway (itemised below). The biggest unknown cost is legal advice, which is **not** in the budget (see the main risk).
- **Path to the first $10k a month.** That needs about $20M of routed taker volume a month at 5 bps, which is 1.6% of Phoenix's current $1.26B per 30 days ([DefiLlama](https://defillama.com/perps/chain/solana)), or roughly 200 to 300 active traders, in line with the Hyperliquid comparables. Base case: $3k to $6k a month by month 12. The $10k a month needs a second venue, adding Hyperliquid's builder-code markets (Entropy, trade.xyz).
- **Why now.** Phoenix launched Flight codes in May 2026 ([aixbt via KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/insight/SOL/6a078285d064730007b5da6d)). It listed an **ANTHROPIC pre-IPO perp on 20 to 21 Oct** and runs a $25k trading campaign around it ([PhoenixTrade](https://x.com/PhoenixTrade/status/2113004091320553976), [solana_defi](https://x.com/solana_defi/status/2111882741746892875)). The Claude Duet and Anthropic IPO talk is the top AI story on X today. I found no third-party Flight frontend.
- **Edge, risk and first move.** The edge is the headline-to-market mapping with a rumour check, on a venue no X overlay serves yet. Raindrop, Xeet and Merit cover Hyperliquid cashtags only. **The main risk is regulatory:** running a frontend that offers leveraged equity and pre-IPO derivatives from Australia may count as a financial service needing an AFSL, so get a paid legal opinion before any real-money launch. **First move this week:** build a read-only overlay (headline to markets, plus the source check, no trading) and measure clicks.
- **Verdict: test, about 35% confidence.** The money model is proven and verifiable on-chain, and the venue is open, but the legal question and thin Phoenix volume could each kill it.

## Problem and user
**User.** A Solana-native trader in Ethan's audience who already trades memecoins on Axiom or GMGN, holds USDC, follows AI news on X, and has started trading "the AI story" as markets. Their options are pre-IPO perps (Anthropic on Phoenix and on Entropy, a Hyperliquid HIP-3 venue) and 24/7 stock perps (NVDA, IREN, CRWV, PLTR on Phoenix and trade.xyz).

**How they do it today.** They read a post, open a second tab with Phoenix, Hyperliquid or trade.xyz, find the ticker, ask @grok "is this real?" (people literally did this under the IREN post: [JPINVESTS23](https://x.com/JPINVESTS23/status/2111890817715458191)), then size and enter by hand. Each step costs time in a market that reprices within minutes. Rumour trades also cost money when the story is false: IREN round-tripped from about $26 to $44 and back to about $26 over a weekend, on reports that "remain unconfirmed by either side" ([grok reply](https://x.com/grok/status/2111890962284732823)).

**What it costs them.** I can't source per-user losses; that's an evidence gap. The sourced proxies are trading spend: users pay Hyperliquid frontends 0.009% to 0.206% of notional on top of exchange fees for convenience ([CleanSky](https://cleansky.io/blog/hyperliquid-builder-codes-based-trade-xyz-distribution-2026/)), and pay Cielo $59 to $199 a month for wallet tracking and alerts ([Cielo](https://cielo.finance/)).

## Product and user experience
**First minute.** Install the extension (or open the Telegram bot), connect Phantom or Solflare, and approve one session key with "trade only, max $X a day, no withdrawals". Under any AI headline on X a small strip appears, for example: `ANTHROPIC +0.0% · IREN −3.1% · CRWV · [Source: single source, unconfirmed]`. Tapping "Long $100 IREN 3x" places a Phoenix taker order with the Flight code attached. A fill card and PnL show inline, and closing is one tap.

**Under the hood (advanced but invisible).**
- An entity-to-market graph: companies, labs, products, people and suppliers map to listed perps. For example "Anthropic" maps to ANTHROPIC, plus its cloud and chip partners only when the post says so. It's built from Phoenix's market list and an LLM pass over the post with a hand-checked mapping table. The confidence score is shown.
- Source check: the claim is matched against primary sources (company X accounts, official blogs, SEC filings, Reuters and Bloomberg headlines through free RSS) and labelled confirmed, single-source or denied, with links. It **never** says "buy".
- The session key comes from a Privy or Turnkey embedded wallet with a policy that only allows the Phoenix program, a per-day notional cap, and withdrawals only to the user's main wallet. Both vendors document program allowlists and spend limits ([Privy](https://docs.privy.io/controls/policies/overview), [Turnkey](https://www.turnkey.com/pricing)). Phoenix recommends one embedded wallet per user ([Flight docs](https://docs.phoenix.trade/builder-codes)).
- The executor uses the Rise SDK in TypeScript with the Flight builder authority, Phoenix's WebSocket order book and mark price, and pre-trade liquidation estimates from the `place-isolated-market-order-enhanced` endpoint.

**MVP (4 weeks, solo with AI coding tools).**
- Week 1: Phoenix Rise SDK on devnet or with small sizes, Flight builder registration, embedded-wallet session key with policy.
- Week 2: Chrome extension (MV3) that reads the visible post text, plus the mapping service (Node with Postgres, LLM calls only for new headlines).
- Week 3: source checker using RSS feeds and official accounts, the Telegram bot, and the fill and PnL cards.
- Week 4: a public dashboard of builder-fee revenue read from the chain, a geoblock, terms, and a closed beta.

**What's genuinely hard.** Keeping the mapping precise, so it never pushes a trade on a wrong ticker. Latency from headline to strip in under 2 seconds. Running a fair source check without paid news APIs. Thin Phoenix order books on smaller equity perps, where slippage warnings have to be shown.

**What a copycat could clone in a weekend.** The trade button and the Flight integration. They can't easily clone the tuned mapping table, the track record of correct and incorrect rumour labels, or the distribution.

## Token design
- **Job in the product.** (1) **Fee tier:** staking 50k $TAPE (an illustrative number) lowers your builder fee from 5 to 2.5 bps. The discount is enforced by the backend setting a lower Flight fee for that wallet if per-order fees are supported; this must be verified, and the fallback is a rebate in $TAPE from the marketing budget, not from revenue. (2) **Access:** stakers get zero-delay strips, while free users see strips 30 seconds late. (3) **Spend:** deep source checks (multi-source, with an archived evidence pack) cost a small $TAPE amount, and spent tokens are burned. These are usage burns, not revenue-funded buybacks.
- **Launch route.** **Bags** (Solana), because it links the mint to the creator's X account at launch, which matters after today's impersonator problems (Mint Seal research), and creator fees go to a public wallet. pump.fun is the alternative, at about the same cost.
- **How fees flow and how to verify them.** Builder fees accrue as collateral on the Flight builder trader account on-chain ([Flight docs](https://docs.phoenix.trade/builder-codes)). A public dashboard reads that account and the Bags creator-fee wallet. Product revenue (builder fees) and token income (creator fees) are reported separately.
- **Public repo from day one.** The extension, the mapping table and the dashboard are open; the scoring prompts and the API keys are not.
- **Comparables.** Bankr's BNKR is a token tied to a trading product on X. Based (BASED) launched in March 2026 and got one revenue rebound month, then kept falling ([CleanSky](https://cleansky.io/blog/hyperliquid-builder-codes-based-trade-xyz-distribution-2026/)). Trench Bot found CrawlHive, a real, used product, sits at about $20k market cap, so **product use doesn't make the token rise**. Don't plan income from token price. X bot flagged $FRENS (a trading-terminal coin) as shilled with templated posts, so give the token a job and skip paid shilling.
- **Legal line.** No revenue sharing, buybacks from revenue, staking yield or profit language. Staking only changes the fee tier and access. It's still a utility token attached to a **derivatives** product, which raises the risk profile; see Risks.

## Business model and unit economics
- **Price:** 5 bps builder fee on taker fills, compared with Hyperliquid builders' effective rates of 0.009% (Axiom) to 0.206% (pvp.trade), with MetaMask at 0.085% ([CleanSky](https://cleansky.io/blog/hyperliquid-builder-codes-based-trade-xyz-distribution-2026/)). All-in cost to the user is 8.5 bps per taker trade, against about 4.5 bps base taker on Hyperliquid plus that venue's builder fee.
- **Cost to serve:** about A$95 a month in fixed costs (estimate). Variable cost is close to zero per trade, plus LLM calls per unique headline (not per user).
- **Gross margin:** over 90% after about $1.5k a month in revenue (estimate).
- **Acquisition:** X (the product lives where the headline is), Phoenix's Flight partner channel and campaigns (the $25k Anthropic campaign shows Phoenix pays for flow), and Solana trader Telegram groups. Paid ads aren't needed and may be barred for derivatives.
- **Payback:** no paid acquisition is planned; payback is immediate per trade.
