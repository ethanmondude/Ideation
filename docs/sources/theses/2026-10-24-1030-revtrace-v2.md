> **Headline finding of this run:** across the 7 most-funded pump.fun Tokenized Agents with measurable deposits (123.75 SOL), **0% of the money came from payers shown to be independent customers**. 51% was the coin's own creator fees routed back by pump.fun's fee sharing, 44% came from the agent's own authority wallet, and 5% came from unattributed single wallets (the most outside revenue could be). All Tokenized Agent buybacks together were **about 30 SOL (~US$3.1k) in ~37 days**, so v1's Tokenized-Agent beachhead fails v1's own kill line (US$50k per 30 days). What survives is one label traders can act on in a second: **where the money behind a "buyback" or "revenue" claim actually came from.** That's a feature, and it fits Clean Feed better than a standalone coin.

## Executive summary
- **Problem:** pump.fun product coins sell "buybacks" and "agent revenue", and traders can't tell outside revenue from their own recycled trading fees or a dev top-up. In this run's test, **0% of the money behind the 7 most-funded Tokenized Agents came from payers shown to be independent**: 51% was recycled creator fees, 44% the agent's own authority wallet, ≤5% unattributed. Traders keep asking "where are the buybacks?" (84 explicit posts in 7 days).
- **Product:** "Receipts", a one-line label on any pump.fun coin's buyback or revenue claim: `buybacks 30d 19.1 SOL · outside payers 0% · recycled creator fees ~100% · team top-ups 0%`, every figure linked to Solscan. It shows as a chip in Clean Feed on X, `/rev <CA>` in Telegram, and an x402/MCP endpoint for agents.
- **Who pays and how much:** traders, through Clean Feed Pro (US$9 a month or hold $CFEED, Clean Feed's plan); agents at **US$0.01 per receipt** over x402 (between botsrated's US$0.001 and Token Verdict's US$0.02). No team verification fees.
- **Token job:** recommended: no separate coin. Deep traces (5-hop funder graph plus evidence pack) **burn the user's own $CFEED**. Standalone fallback: Project Bot's $TRACE plan on pump.fun as is (hold for Pro, burn own on deep traces, Holder Rewards and Tokenized Agent off, no flag bond).
- **Total cost vs A$1,000:** about **A$0-75 a month extra** inside Clean Feed (Helius only past the free tier), sharing its A$300 legal consult. Standalone: ~A$335 to day 30, ~A$695 over 4 months (review and v1).
- **Path to first US$10k a month:** not credible standalone (bear ~US$0.3k, base ~US$1.2k MRR by month 12, estimates). It contributes to Clean Feed's route: Receipts as a Pro reason to upgrade, plus x402 (1M receipts a month at US$0.01 = US$10k; that needs agent demand nobody has shown yet).
- **Why now, edge, main risk:** pump.fun's fee sharing and Tokenized Agents (2026) made fee-funded "buybacks" automatic and common, and both are readable on-chain in seconds. Edge: the only label that classifies the **source** of the money (CFO shows where fees go, not who funded a buyback). Main risk: copyable in weeks by CFO, SolEnrich or pumpdata.fun, and multi-hop attribution is often "unattributed" ($CLAWD).
- **First move this week (free, private):** hand-label the payer class on 40 receipts (the 8 agents plus 32 self-posted buyback accounts from X bot's data); a second bot blind-labels 30; pass at 85% agreement. Then a head-to-head against CFO's check and SolEnrich on the same coins.
- **Verdict: pass as a standalone product and coin (about 70% confident); test as Clean Feed's "Receipts" feature (about 30% it earns a permanent place).**

## Innovation, usability, uniqueness
- **Innovation: 5/10.** Why it's new now: pump.fun's creator-fee sharing (one-time `sharing_config`, up to 10 wallets) and Tokenized Agents (2026) route trading fees into "buybacks" automatically, through `DistributeCreatorFees` transactions anyone can read. A year ago there was no standard fee-routing to classify, and "agent revenue" didn't exist on pump.fun. The tracing itself (funder graphs) is old.
- **Usability: first use in under 30 seconds, 5 steps, inside X (Clean Feed) or Telegram:**
  1. Install Clean Feed (or open the Telegram bot). No wallet, no X login.
  2. Scroll to any coin post that says "buyback", "burn" or "revenue".
  3. The chip under it already reads `Receipts: 0% outside payers · ~100% recycled creator fees`.
  4. Tap it: 3 lines with Solscan links (claimed vs done, source split, last buyback).
  5. Optional: tap "deep trace" (burns your own tokens) for the 5-hop evidence pack.
  - Honest limit: coins already indexed answer instantly; a cold coin needs a background trace of about 30-60 seconds on a paid RPC (this run's public-RPC test took minutes).
- **Uniqueness: 5/10.** Closest rival: **CFO's Buyback check** (free, any pump.fun coin; shows where the creator fee goes and whether anyone can change it). **The one thing we do that it doesn't:** classify who actually funded each buyback or deposit (outside customer, recycled creator fees, team wallet, unattributed), including buybacks done by hand, which CFO says "aren't something it can see". Not higher: CFO, SolEnrich or pumpdata.fun hold the data and could add it in weeks.
- **Trench Bot's 10-second real-or-fake test:** paste a CA, get "is this buyback outside money or recycled fees?" with no wallet connected. Passes for indexed coins (~1 second, cached); fails today for cold coins (30-60 s). Scored on indexed coins only until a paid RPC is in.

## Product and UX
**First minute:** see the 5-step flow above. The card:
`$RKC · Receipts (30d): buybacks 19.1 SOL · outside payers 0% · recycled creator fees ~100% (pump.fun fee sharing) · team top-ups 0% · claimed vs done: no claim found · volume: no wash flags` → each part links to transactions.

**MVP stack:** a TypeScript indexer on Clean Feed's existing VPS; Helius (free tier, then Developer US$49) or public RPC with caching; Postgres (Supabase free); the Clean Feed extension renders the chip; grammY Telegram bot; x402 endpoint (Solana USDC) plus an MCP listing.

**Week-by-week (solo, ~10-12 h a week on top of Clean Feed):**
- **Week 1:** index all Tokenized Agent accounts (getProgramAccounts, done in this run), the buyback crank's history and pump.fun fee-sharing distributions (`DistributeCreatorFees`, `TransferCreatorFeesToPump`). Classify inflows: fee distributor → recycled; agent authority or creator → team; else → trace 2 hops.
- **Week 2:** hand-built claim parser for self-posted receipts ("Buyback #N … for X SOL", "% reinvested") on X, using X bot's backfill; claimed vs done.
- **Week 3:** chip in Clean Feed, `/rev` in Telegram, x402 endpoint; open methodology page with versioned rules (copy x402stats' style).
- **Week 4:** Squads integrity page as a private dashboard first; public only with Ethan's OK and after the legal check.

**Advanced tech under the hood:** decoding the agent program from the published IDL; a funder graph shared with Clean Feed (copycat deployers, caller-ring wallets); wSOL/USDC flow reconciliation per crank cycle; CEX hot-wallet list so CEX funders show as "unattributed".

**What's hard:** attribution beyond 2 hops ($CLAWD's funder chain ends at an unknown wallet); keeping false "team" labels near zero; claim parsing from free-text posts; public RPC rate limits (this run's fetches ran ~2-8 transactions a second).

**What's clonable:** everything except the labelled funder archive and the record of correct calls. CFO, SolEnrich or pumpdata.fun could ship the basic split in weeks.

**Agent distribution:** x402 endpoint `GET /receipts/<mint>` at US$0.01, MCP server listed on Glama and the MCP Registry, and a Grok Bot template.

## Token design on pump.fun
**Recommended: no separate coin.** Receipts lives inside Clean Feed. Its token job there: **deep traces burn the user's own $CFEED** (hold for Pro already covers the chip). One coin, one CA, no referee-holds-its-own-coin conflict on a second ticker.

**Standalone fallback, Project Bot's plan from section 5 of the review, as is:**
- **Job:** (1) hold $TRACE for Pro alerts instead of US$19 a month; (2) spend $TRACE on deep traces (5-hop graph plus evidence pack), burned on use; (3) teams can pay verification in $TRACE. **No flag bond.** All USDC income goes to a public wallet, and Revtrace lists itself on its own leaderboard.
- **Launch route: pump.fun.** No platform fee to create; bonding curve 1.25% per trade, of which 0.30% is the creator fee; PumpSwap creator fee tiered by market cap (0.30% under 420 SOL, 0.95% at 420-1,470 SOL, then stepping down); traders pay pump.fun's 0.45% Platform Fee on app trades, none to the creator ([fees](https://pump.fun/docs/fees)). Settings: SOL pair, **Holder Rewards off**, mint and freeze authority confirmed revoked on-chain, no bundled wallets, **Tokenized Agent off** (it's a revenue-funded buyback, and this run shows such buybacks are mostly recycled creator fees). CA posted from the official X account at launch, the X link in socials, mint and creator wallet shown on the site, and the creator-fee wallet listed on its own leaderboard. Open scoring repo and self-listing are must-haves.
- **Creator fees: plan on zero.** At US$5k a day of volume the creator fee would be ~US$15 a day under 420 SOL market cap (review estimate).
- **When:** on the day a census report goes out, only after the legal review and at least 10 paying users.
- **Ethan's rules applied on top:** small labelled dev buy, no bundles, pin one official CA on day one, no revenue share, no revenue- or creator-fee-funded buybacks or burns, no profit language.

**Token fit (my estimate): standalone 5/10 (unchanged from the review). Merged: inherits Clean Feed's (its v1 estimate 6/10).**
