## Product and UX
**First minute (AT-23: <30 s, ≤5 steps, no sign-up, wallet or X login):**
1. Add to Chrome. Toast: "Marshal is on · checks locally · only CAs leave your browser".
2. On x.com, every CA, $ticker and token link gets a pill (grey fact, amber warning, red rule hit; never green). Lure replies fold: `Hidden · airdrop lure ("check memepage") · Show · Not spam`.
3. A post brags "$MIL JUST HIT $10.6M MC" → `claimed $10.6M · live $427k (DexScreener, 14:29)`.
4. Click a reply's chart link to GMGN → one-time site permission → if the mint differs: `⚠ Not the mint in the post you came from (@author, 1 min ago) · Why · Dismiss`. The buy panel is untouched.
5. (Optional, later) Popup → "Led you here" → paste a public wallet address → "Tracking from today. Nothing leaves your browser." From the next buy on, accounts that sent you get a small stamp: `led you here 3× · −1.4 SOL · 1 of 3 held 24h`.

**Led-you-here: how it reads.** It's always *your* result, never a verdict on the account. Lines: "you're −1.4 SOL across 2 coins from this account" (realized + marked to market, labelled); "1 fell 80% within 1h of your buy"; "1 of 3 held 24h" (24h after your buy, price ≥50% of your entry). No number out of 10, no colour above amber, never uploaded, and the "Why" panel shows each buy with its post link and Solscan tx. A history button backfills only **back to your install day**, because Marshal has no clicks from before then. That removes the 20–60 min sync Git bot measured for a full 30-day pull.

**Advanced tech:** post → terminal provenance through `chrome.storage.session` (guard) and an IndexedDB click log (90 days, local); owner-based swap matching on the user's own RPC; X snowflake dating for R11; jsQR in an OffscreenCanvas worker (last in v1); gain-line ring graph with relay-pair edges.

**What's hard:** X DOM churn (fail open); terminal URL formats (Jupiter, Axiom, Photon unverified); referrer after the t.co hop; matching swaps through routers (match on token-balance owner, not signer; USDC-routed buys; non-SOL quotes → "unknown"); public RPC 429s (Git bot: 429s after a few calls at ~1 call per 3 s; idea bot: 0×429 on 51 calls), so offer a "use your own free Helius key" field.

**Clonable:** EDGERUN could add provenance and claimed-MC; Frontrun or Axiom could join X data to PnL. **Not clonable:** each user's own click history (it starts at their Marshal install), the ring rules tuned on Trench Bot's and X bot's labelled rings, and the read-only, no-wallet-connect trust story.

**Scores (mine; Project Bot scores officially), with change from v3's official scores:** **Innovation 7 (+1)**: personal post-to-trade attribution on device is new, and its pieces (owner-matched swap parsing, click provenance) became cheap only recently. **Usability 8 (=)**: first value <30 s is unchanged; led-you-here is a one-paste opt-in later; no mobile. **Uniqueness 6.5 (+1.5)**: head-to-head passed (6) plus an unclaimed lead with only Partial rivals. Closest rival: EDGERUN for the feed, Frontrun for attribution. What we do that they can't: the guard's click memory.

## Token design on pump.fun ($MRSHL)
- **v1 job: hold to unlock Pro.** Website `signMessage` nonce (never a transaction, never in the extension) → server reads the SPL balance via Helius → 30-day Pro token, re-checked on renewal.
- **v1.1 job: burn your own per use.** (a) **Agents** using the Marshal skill (ElizaOS plugin + MCP tool) prepay by signing an SPL `burn` of their own $MRSHL; the server checks mint, amount, owner and signature on-chain and credits N calls; a public ledger links every credit to its burn tx. (b) **Humans** burn for deep deployer-farm traces. x402 can't do this, because it transfers to `payTo`, so it's a separate flow.
- **USDC path:** agents may instead pay US$0.01/call in USDC over x402. That's **plain income for hosting and the business. It's never swapped into $MRSHL, never burned and never distributed.** Card revenue is treated the same.
- **Never:** holder payouts, revenue share, staking yield, revenue- or creator-fee-funded buybacks or burns, profit promises.
- **Launch rules:** pump.fun only, **after** the first paying users (≥25 Pro or ≥30% of installs active 3+ days a week) plus the legal consult, and only once Ethan picks Marshal. **Mint and freeze authority off** (confirm on-chain). **Small labelled dev buy ≤0.5 SOL** from a published wallet, no-sell policy (est. 90 days). **No bundles, snipers or paid callers.** **Holder Rewards OFF, Tokenized Agent buybacks OFF.** **Pin one official CA on day one** (site, X, extension settings), and Marshal stamps $MRSHL copies "not the pinned CA" from minute one. Don't send supply to a famous wallet or link a famous post (Trench Bot run 14).
- **Fee flow and verification** ([pump.fun fees](https://pump.fun/docs/fees), updated 17 Oct 2026): 0 create fee; 1.25% curve fee with 0.30% to the creator; 0.45% app Platform Fee; PumpSwap creator fee tiers by market cap. Creator fees go to a **published operating wallet used for hosting only**, verifiable on Solscan. **Creator fees are a bonus, never the plan.**
- **Public repo:** extension, Worker and burn-credit verifier are MIT, so anyone can confirm there's no wallet access, no post-text upload and no USDC→$MRSHL swap code.
- **Comparables:** allowed: Bubblemaps (hold-to-unlock), Larpcheck's original hold-to-access. Barred: Buddy (80% fee buyback-burn), Raindrop (50% swap fees), fable (50% API revenue), Larpcheck's claimed 60% buyback, Merit's "trade to earn" tokenomics (coming soon, unspecified). Organic vs shilled attention is not measured for any of them.
- **Legal line (not legal advice; the A$300 consult signs off):** ASIC [INFO 225](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/digital-transformation/digital-assets-financial-products-and-services): rights decide status, not labels. Never say returns, price, "burns raise the price", "not a financial product" or "ASIC-compliant". No paid promoters (INFO 269). No token talk on the CWS listing.
- **Token fit (my est.): 6.5–7** (v3: 6). Two plain use jobs (hold for access, burn own per use), with no cash ever flowing into the token.

### 5. Core logic
**Guard (gated):**
```
on x.com click(a) where a.href hosts in TERMINALS or post has CA:
   m = mint_from_url(a.href) or post.raw_ca or link_text_ca      # never fetch t.co
   session.clicks.push({m, postId, author, ts:now, tab}); idb.clicks.put(same)   # idb kept 90 d
on terminal route load:
   p = resolve(location.pathname); c = newest click for this tab
   fire RED only if c && now - c.ts <= 120s && first_route_since(c) && (referrer in {t.co, x.com} || referrer == "")
      && p != c.m            -> "⚠ Not the mint in the post you came from (@{c.author}, {age})"
   never touch page elements, cookies, storage, wallet or terminal APIs
```
**Led-you-here (on device):**
```
opt-in: wallets[] = user-pasted base58 pubkeys (≤3); start_slot = now; rpc = user_key ? helius : public
after each recorded click c on mint m: schedule polls at +2, +10, +30 min (chrome.alarms)
poll(w): sigs = getSignaturesForAddress(w, {until: w.last_sig, limit: 25}); w.last_sig = sigs[0]
   for s in sigs: tx = getTransaction(s, {encoding:"jsonParsed", maxSupportedTransactionVersion: 1})
      d = post - pre token balance for (owner == w, mint == m)               # owner, never signer
      if d > 0 and 0 <= tx.blockTime - c.ts <= 1800:
          spent = SOL out of w (minus fees), or USDC out → USD; other quote → entry = "unknown"
          buy = {m, tokens:d, spent, entry: spent/d, t: blockTime, post: c.postId, author: c.author}
          attribute to the most recent click on m before t; other clicks on m → "also seen from"
outcomes per buy (cached, re-checked at +1h, +24h, then daily while held):
   crash_1h = min(GeckoTerminal 5-min low in [t, t+1h]) <= 0.2 * entry_usd
   held_24h = price(t+24h) >= 0.5 * entry_usd
   sol_result = SOL in from w's sells of m − spent  (+ held tokens × DexScreener price, labelled "marked")
stamp(author) shown only if ≥1 buy: "led you here N× · {±X.X SOL} · {k} of N held 24h" ; "{j} fell 80% within 1h of your buy" in Why
never: upload wallet/buys, score 0–10, colour above amber, show on accounts with 0 matched buys
backfill button: same loop from install_day (clicks don't exist earlier), with an ETA and cancel
```
**Rings + new edges:**
```
gain_line ring rule from v3 (3+ accounts / 10 min / one CA = edge; ring = 3+ accts each with edges on 2+ CAs)
relay pair: post q by b ~ post p by a (normalised text Jaccard ≥0.8 or same numbers sequence), 0 < t_q − t_p ≤ 30 min, same CA
      → treat as one source for burst counts; add edge(a,b) weight 1 per CA
linked to ring: account x shares ≥2 windows with members of known ring R on ≥1 CA but fails the 2-CA rule
      → grey "linked to ring R (1 coin)", never member, never red; decays in 14 d
```
**R11 borrowed endorsement:** `post_ms = (id >> 22) + 1288834974657`; if a coin's linked X post `post_ms < mint_created_ms` and its text names neither the ticker nor the CA → amber "linked post is {d} days older than this coin and doesn't name it". Peekhole's 1-minute-older post names Peekhole, so it stays silent.

**Agent skill (v1.1):**
```
POST /v1/skill/post-mint {post_url, mint, text?}
  text = text ?? oEmbed(post_url).html → strip → extract CAs, tickers, claimed MC
  return {match: mint in post CAs, post_cas, author, posted_at (snowflake), family_line, claimed_vs_live, r11}
pay: header X-MRSHL-Credit (burn-backed credits) → decrement; else x402 402-offer US$0.01 USDC → verify → serve
burn credits: verify tx: SPL Burn, mint == MRSHL, owner == caller wallet, amount ≥ price×N, sig unused → credit N
USDC: lands in the operating wallet; NO code path swaps USDC (or SOL) into MRSHL
```
