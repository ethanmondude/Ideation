## Executive summary
- **Lead job.** On every Solana contract address (CA), $ticker or token link in the X feed, replies and Communities, Clean Feed **automatically** stamps one of four verdicts, with no paste and no click:
  - **real $X**;
  - **copy #7 of 9 of $X → real: 25X5…pump**;
  - **no confirmed real mint**;
  - **new, unverified**.

  It also **collapses the shill and phishing replies around that CA** with a one-line reason ("same text as 14 other replies in 3 min"). General scam and fluff filtering supports the lead but isn't the lead.
- **Problem.**
  - Copycat mints arrive by the dozen. Trench Bot logged **215 brand name-bait mints among 314 new $500k coins in about 25 hours** (SpaceX 17, IShowSpeed 11, YouTube 11, Gemini 11, MrBeast 10).
  - Caller rings and "airdrop page / 10% supply / allocation scan" phishing ride every hot coin. In X bot's 119 shill checks, **44 of the 48 shilled or mixed coins carried a pattern the extension could collapse in the page** (this run's free test).
  - Traders can't tell which CA in a reply is the real one.
- **Headline feature: the automatic copycat-mint stamp.** The collapse bar only supports it. [BotBlind](https://botblind.app/) (free, launched 21 Oct) already ships a reason bar with Show and Not-a-bot buttons and 3 strictness modes, so the bar alone caps uniqueness at 4 (Project Bot).
- **Rivals to beat: [Raindrop](https://raindropext.com/docs), [fable](https://fable.market/) and BotBlind.**
  - fable is free and open source on x.com only. It puts a scam/caution/trusted line under crypto posts, adds SCAM/RUG/SHILL stamps, CA and $ticker underlines, and a profile backstory panel. It has 57 Chrome users, is Robinhood-Chain-first, pays $FABLE stakers from revenue, and uploads scrolled posts by default.
  - Raindrop already detects every CA, pump.fun mint and ticker in the X timeline, with price, RugCheck data and one-tap buy.
  - [PastTokenBot](https://pasttokenbot.fun/) already lists older same-ticker tokens, but only when you paste a CA on its website.
  - BotBlind collapses spam, scam and AI-bot replies, but by its own description it has no contract or crypto-template checks (untested).
  - **Nobody stamps "real or copy #N of $X" automatically on every CA in the feed.**
- **Who pays and the token's job.** Desktop memecoin and perps traders. The core is free. Pro is US$9/month **or held $CFEED**, and unlocks unlimited automatic verdicts, Communities safe mode and copycat alerts on coins you hold. Agents pay x402 at US$0.005 per check. **Token = hold to unlock access, and burn your own for deep checks and credits. That's all.** No holder payouts, no revenue buybacks, and no paying anyone from slashed stakes.
- **Cost against A$1,000.** About **A$72 to day 30 and A$545 over 4 months** (estimates), including the A$300 legal consult and the A$83 launch. Server, RPC and LLM spend has a **hard cap of A$25 a month**; above it the extension falls back to local-only folding. RobinSight, a token-backed X scanner, shut down because of server costs.
- **Main risks.**
  - fable and Raindrop are one release away from a copycat badge, and BotBlind could add crypto rules.
  - Nobody has been shown paying a subscription for an X scam filter. Security extensions earn from swap fees (Scam Sniffer 0.25%; Pocket Universe and Kerberus 0.8-0.95%), and that's execution, which our rules avoid.
  - X's ToS (effective 9 Oct 2026) bans scraping.
  - X DOM changes will break things.
  - There's no mobile app support.
  - A single wrong "real" call that drains someone would sink trust.
- **Proof plan.** v1 only counts as beating fable if it passes **all three blind sets**: Mint Seal's frozen holdout, Trench Bot's 182 held-back cases, and Project Bot's 60-post feed set. The pass lines are below. The hard cases get their own line in the report.
- **Verdict: test, about 30%.** The lead is the right shape for tech bros: instant, automatic, inside X, read-only. But it's a narrow edge with three fast rivals next to it, and no payer shown yet. Gate c (crowded) and gate e (money) are shaky.

## Innovation, usability, uniqueness
- **Demand you can see (X bot, public posts):**
  - [@alexrkonrad](https://x.com/alexrkonrad/status/2111884597315178562) (91k followers) and [@AdamLowisz](https://x.com/AdamLowisz/status/2111901665800388919) (55k) complain about the scam and spam layer on X.
  - [@kimiloo](https://x.com/kimiloo/status/2113311612534972456): "drainer links under literally every tweet".
  - X bot's demand.jsonl has 20 posts: 16 trader complaints, workarounds, wishes or builders, and 3 projects warning "beware of fake tokens".
  - This is demand for relief, not proof that anyone pays.
- **Innovation: 6/10.** Why it's possible now:
  - Same-ticker copies now arrive by the dozen within hours (Trench Bot run 10).
  - pumpdata.fun gives free deployer history and launch-buyer counts with no key.
  - Cheap Solana RPC reads give mint age, authorities, holders and pool reserves in about a second.
  - Caller rings reuse the same accounts across coins: 25 of 26 named ring accounts recur in X bot's records.

  A year ago you couldn't build an automatic, per-CA "real or copy #N" answer inside the feed: the copycat volume and the free per-mint data weren't there. The collapse UI isn't new (BotBlind, fable).
- **Usability: 8/10** (Git bot's score; first use under 30 seconds inside X):
  1. Click "Add to Chrome".
  2. Open x.com. No sign-up, wallet or X login needed.
  3. **Every CA in view already shows its stamp**, e.g. "copy #3 of 5 of $AOP · real: 25X5…pump · deployer 41 launches, 0 graduated". Click it for the "why" panel.
  4. Open that post's replies. The shill and phishing replies around the CA are already collapsed, e.g. "fake airdrop link · same text as 9 others".
  5. Flip filtered/raw, or drag the strictness slider.

  Minus: mobile isn't supported, since there are no extensions in the X app.
- **Uniqueness: 5/10.** Project Bot scores 3/10 for the broad filter, about 6/10 narrowed, and caps the collapse bar alone at 4 because BotBlind ships it. Git bot scores 5.
  - **Closest rivals:**
    - [fable](https://fable.market/) gives a per-contract risk line in the feed.
    - [Raindrop](https://raindropext.com/docs) auto-detects every CA and ticker in the timeline, as a trading overlay.
    - [PastTokenBot](https://pasttokenbot.fun/) gives a same-ticker list on paste.
  - **The one thing we do that none of them ships:** an **automatic** ticker-family verdict on every CA in the feed ("copy #N of $X, real is …"). We also collapse the shill and phishing replies around it **without condemning a coin that's holding up** (Kimchi, CHOO, ヤギ).
  - Why not 6: PastTokenBot already does the comparison, and fable or Raindrop could ship the badge in a release.
- **Trench Bot's 10-second test** (paste or hover a CA or link → clear real-or-fake answer in under 10s, no wallet): **designed to pass, not yet measured.** The target is under 2s for a cached mint and under 8s cold (estimate), timed in the blind runs.

## Problem and user
- **User:** tech-bro traders and builders who find coins on CT and in X Communities on desktop Chrome, Brave or Arc, next to Axiom, GMGN or Photon. Also caller-group admins and Community mods.
- **Design data (Trench Bot, 398 design cases; results on it are method checks only):**
  - **Fake CAs:** 87, including 15 where the real mint is known (Kimchi, AOP, PHP families).
  - **Brand-name coins:** 210. These are easy, with many near-duplicates (17 SpaceX mints).
  - **Phishing accounts:** 13 ("airdrop page" and "10% supply / check MemePage" templates).
  - **Shill-ring accounts:** 59, plus 9 paid-attention coins.
  - **Non-scam cases:** 20 (7 coins, 13 accounts).
  - **Gaps:** no confirmed drainer URLs (t.co links only), no Communities data, labels on 51 shill accounts are low confidence, and **20 non-scams against roughly 370 scams can't show false-flag rates.**
- **Hard cases:**
  - The "airdrop page" and "10% supply" spam also appears on coins that held up (Kimchi, CHOO, ヤギ, WRHND). Clean Feed must **collapse the spam reply and leave the coin's stamp alone**.
  - Scam-warning accounts (@RaOnChain, @allenson5002, @StiffMem) must stay visible.
  - A large KOL is labelled as paid promo. That label is private only.
  - **Fake US fund-name coin farms** (launched 21 Oct; Trench Bot run 11) show $2-282M caps with no wallet above about 0.6%, so they pass holder and concentration tests. Only an unpaid-poster / paid-attention check catches them.
  - **Risky survivors with all-paid attention**, e.g. $shiit (412ECWXN3jGbd1kn8ck7eeupWttkmrFkNm9JqfAzpump): every X mention is paid callers and reply spam. The stamp should say "risky · attention is paid callers only", not "fake".
  - X bot's genuine hard cases:
    - an organic $KIMCHI "airdrop" post;
    - a single reply carrying the real Kimchi CA;
    - Phantom's "Refer friends, get paid";
    - pump.fun's $2.5M competition post;
    - Solana's "BREAKING: $AAPL is now live";
    - Cointelegraph's Netanyahu story, posted while $NETANYAHU coins were being pushed.
- **Patterns from X bot (public search):**
  - AOP: the AlphaPulsevw, clebers1984 and Davide_Alp5y2 accounts posted identical "$61K → $56.4M 924X" text.
  - $ODIE: one account posted 15+ identical CA replies in 3 minutes.
  - 1SHARE: about 12 aged accounts posted a paired "printing massively on my Alpha telegram" + "164.07x" template.
  - WRHND and YMB: 0-follower accounts posted "10% of the supply is being distributed to holders… check your eligibility on MemePage" ([example](https://x.com/brhimBayrak1/status/2113811245786964189)).
  - Other templates: "allocation scan"; "Next 100x gem loading"; "913x"; "CA=" airdrop text.
  - Farmed hype: $FRENS paid floods, AgentDojo's identical paragraphs.
  - A copycat example from Trench Bot: posts about "Kimchi rugged" were about Project Kimchi (B1Y2…pump), not the main Kimchi.
- **Extension trust is low.** [Crypto Copilot](https://www.coti.news/news/malicious-chrome-extension-skims-solana-swaps-with-hidden-transfers-from-x-traders), an "X swap" extension, secretly skimmed Solana swaps. So: read-only, no wallet permissions, open source.
- **Today's workaround:** paste the CA into PastTokenBot, RugCheck or a terminal; tag @Larpcheckbot or @grok; mute words by hand. Wallets catch drainers only at signing.

## Product and UX
**MVP wedge (2-3 weeks solo; Git bot's estimate): automatic copycat stamp + shill and phishing reply collapse.**

1. **Copycat stamp (Mint Seal inside).**
   - Detects Solana mints (base58, `…pump`), $tickers and DexScreener, pump.fun or Axiom links in the feed, replies and Communities.
   - Lists same-ticker and same-name siblings using pump.fun and DexScreener *search*, for the candidate list only.
   - Decides the verdict from **on-chain reads** plus deployer history:
     - mint creation slot and age;
     - mint and freeze authority;
     - holder count;
     - pool reserves read from the pool account (never DexScreener liquidity, which copycats fake into the billions);
     - number of distinct launch buyers and deployer launch record (pumpdata.fun), not bundle share, since a 79.31% curve buyout is now normal;
     - the CA pinned by the project's own X account, if any;
     - Jupiter's verified tag.
   - **Attention signal:** what share of a mint's X posters are known template or caller accounts versus unpaid organic posters. It's computed locally from the posts in view plus the private ring memory, and adds a **risky · paid attention only** modifier. It's needed because fund-name farms pass every holder test.
   - **Never shows green without two independent signals agreeing.** Otherwise it shows "no confirmed real mint".
   - Handles migrations (SLERF, MYRO: "moved to new mint"). No hardcoded tickers.
   - Labels are text in our own style, never X-like checkmarks.
2. **Reply collapse around the CA.**
   - In-page duplicate detection: N+ accounts posting the same text within M minutes, one account posting the same CA reply 15+ times.
   - Known templates (gain-multiple, "Alpha telegram", "airdrop page", "10% supply / check eligibility", "allocation scan").
   - Link domains checked against MetaMask eth-phishing-detect, scamsniffer/scam-database and phantom/blocklist (licences to confirm), plus lookalike-domain rules.
   - **Reply-level, not coin-level:** collapsing spam under Kimchi doesn't change Kimchi's stamp.
   - Scam-warning replies ("this is a copycat, real CA is…") are kept and boosted, not hidden.
3. **UX.**
   - Collapse, never delete. One-line reason. "Why" panel. Filtered/raw toggle.
   - Strictness slider. Per-account always-show and always-hide, stored locally.
   - Communities safe mode (strict preset).

**Later, after the money test:**
- Tapeline "first reported Xh ago" stamp, off by default until v4 retrieval passes.
- Decoy link sandbox.
- Private paid-promo labels (never public).
- Telegram copycat alerts.
- Firefox.

**Stack and data flow (designed for X's ToS).**
- Chrome MV3, host permission x.com only. **No remotely fetched code**: rules ship as data only.
- **Posts are checked locally.** Only the CA, link domain or handle being checked is sent to our API. Never post text, never bulk uploads, no default "help improve" upload (unlike fable).
- API on a A$10/month VPS (Hono) with a Redis cache per mint, free-tier Solana RPC and pumpdata.fun free.
- **Hard spend cap of A$25/month** across server, RPC and LLM. Checks only run when a CA scrolls into view, and are cached per mint across all users. At the cap, new uncached mints show "unverified" and folding stays local.
- Payments: Lemon Squeezy card, Solana Pay USDC, optional $CFEED hold check by wallet signature (sign-in only, no transaction permissions).

**Week by week (20-25 h/week).**
1. DOM layer, template and duplicate rules, reason line, raw toggle. Method-check on the 398 design cases, reported as method only.
2. Copycat engine: candidate lookup, on-chain reads, verdict rules, cache, cost cap.
3. Link checks, slider, Communities preset, Pro gating, MCP + x402.
4. Unlisted CWS listing. Hand the build to the three blind runs.

**What's hard.**
- X DOM churn.
- Telling a genuinely excited reply from a template.
- Finding "the real one" when the creator never pins a CA (the Kimchi dispute).
- Cross-chain impostors (EVM copies of Solana coins).

## Agent distribution
- **MCP** `check_ca(mint)` → {verdict, real_mint, copy_rank, reasons}, plus `check_link(url)`. Free for 50 checks a day. About 1 day to build.
- **x402 on Solana:** US$0.005 per check, paid in USDC or with burned $CFEED credits. About 2 days.
- **Grok Bot template plus a paste-in prompt** ("is this CA the real $X?"). About half a day.
- **Honest limit:** Rempart, pumpdata.fun, SolEnrich and RugCheck already sell per-mint checks. Only the family verdict is new.

## Token design (pump.fun)
- **Only two jobs:**
  - (1) **Hold** $CFEED to unlock Pro: unlimited automatic stamps, Communities safe mode, copycat alerts. Checked by wallet signature. Card users never need the token.
  - (2) **Burn your own** for deep checks (deployer funding graph, cross-chain impostors) and for API/x402 credits.

**What v2 changes:**

- **Duplicate check:** leading @mentions are stripped first, and it needs 5+ words, so short "whats the ca" questions don't count as copies.
- **Bare-$ticker reply bursts:** caught when one account drops the same ticker in 3+ threads, or 5+ accounts drop it across 5+ threads (so 3 organic fans saying "$KIMCHI 🚀" stay visible).
- **Phishing phrase families:** "may/could be eligible", "eligible on X airdrop + check", "scan your wallet", "claim up to", and a general airdrop-page family.

- **Warning guard:** posts that are warnings, corrections or CA questions never get lure or link rules.
- **Link rules are patterns:**
  - a free static host (netlify.app, vercel.app, pages.dev and similar) with a Solana mint in the URL, or a claim-style subdomain name;

  - the MetaMask list, used only as a secondary check.
  - Links are read only as the expanded URL X shows; pages are never fetched.

  - "no confirmed real mint" is **neutral, never a flag**;
  - it names a real coin only when **2+ signals agree on the same mint**, and at least one is not the Jupiter verified tag;
  - names are matched exactly after stripping suffixes like "(reversed)". There's no substring matching, and a ticker-only match with a conflicting name doesn't count, so ELON never resolves to Dogelon.
