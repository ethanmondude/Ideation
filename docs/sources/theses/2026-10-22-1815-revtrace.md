# Revtrace: who is actually paying? Organic on-chain revenue for Solana product coins

*Idea bot thesis, Thu 22 Oct 2026, 18:15 AWST (Perth). Path: /workspace/idea-bot/theses/2026-10-22-1815-revtrace.md. Working name; I haven't checked the name or domain for conflicts.*

## Executive summary
- **Problem:** "Real revenue" is now the pitch for Solana product coins, but it's self-reported and often manufactured. On PONS, 91.8% of new-token volume was self-funded ([ponsfiles](https://www.ponsfiles.com/)). StonkFun's buyback was claimed at $2.09M; the chain shows $1.23M ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/is-stonkfun-dumping-on-holders)). Pump.fun doesn't verify who deposits into a Tokenized Agent ([docs](https://pump.fun/docs/tokenized-agent-disclaimer)).
- **Product:** Paste a contract address into the site or the Telegram bot and get organic revenue (independent payers) against creator-linked deposits, buybacks claimed against done, and price to organic revenue, every figure linked to its transactions. A leaderboard ranks coins by organic revenue. Log in with X or a wallet.
- **Who pays:** Teams pay US$149 to verify their fee wallets and get a badge (under DexScreener's US$299 profile) and US$299 a month for a labelled featured slot (TrustMRR's price). Traders pay US$19 a month for Pro alerts. Agents pay US$0.01 per API call over x402.
- **Token:** $TRACE on Bags. Hold it for Pro, spend it on deep traces (burned), or post it as a bond to flag suspect revenue (slashed if the flag is wrong). No buybacks or payouts. Revtrace's own fee wallets appear on its own leaderboard.
- **Cost:** About A$610 for 3 months (A$695 for 4), which includes a A$300 legal reserve. The DexScreener profile waits until there's revenue.
- **Path to US$10k MRR:** 6 slots ($1.8k) + 25 verifications a month ($3.7k) + 200 Pro users ($3.8k) + API ($0.7k). Base case is about US$8.5k by month 12; bear case US$1.5k.
- **Why now and the edge:** Tokenized Agents (March 2026) and revenue coins are producing claims faster than anyone checks them, and the checks so far (ponsfiles, Bitquery) are one-off reports. Gem Screener and ICM take revenue as reported. Revtrace's edge is checking whether the payers are independent of the team.
- **Main risk and first move:** Teams may not pay to be scrutinised, and a false "self-funded" label risks defamation. This week: a 3-day spike counting all Tokenized Agent deposits and their payers, then a draft of Revenue Census #1 for Ethan to approve.

**Verdict: test, about 40% confidence.** Paying for verified revenue is proven next door (TrustMRR: about US$300k in 9 months, self-reported). The open question is whether coin teams pay.

## Problem and user
**User 1, the trader (free and Pro).** A Solana tech bro who now buys "revenue coins" (product tokens with fees, buybacks or agent revenue) and wants to know whether the revenue is real before buying in size. Today they rely on the project's own dashboard (Veil, copyfomo, StonkFun /revenue), a DefiLlama page if the coin has one, or a reply asking an AI bot. Sample asks on X: "Onchain proof of revenue I can actually verify myself" ([TiTan_Web3_](https://x.com/TiTan_Web3_/status/2108070120266563955)); "Dashboards mean little without direct onchain proof of revenue" ([HilaryGray789](https://x.com/HilaryGray789/status/2102386064027447352)); "are buybacks gonna be visible onchain per week or just a total" ([madeby_neo](https://x.com/madeby_neo/status/2112472777932325312)). A forensics trader had a bot pick apart Veil's "revenue is live" post: "0.3% fee … → 2-of-2 team multisig. Not buybacks. Not holders" ([0nchain_frenzy](https://x.com/0nchain_frenzy/status/2112911972379349190), quoting [Veil](https://x.com/BrotherMKT/status/2112916396040433854): "$61,387 earned in the first week verifiable on chain").

**User 2, the honest team (payer).** A builder who launched a product coin (Tokenized Agent, Bags or Believe app, StonkFun-style launchpad, x402 seller) and needs strangers to believe the revenue. Today they hand-build dashboards (GitHub has one-coin "fee-flow audit" and buyback dashboards pushed this week, e.g. [brodie-terminal](https://github.com/Zhaohh0706/brodie-terminal)), pay US$299 for a DexScreener profile, or pay for reach: X bot found TermiX revenue claims pushed by a reply campaign, with figures that disagree with each other, and AXIS paying 0.25% of supply to Kaito leaderboards (X bot 6pm digest).

**What the problem costs.**
- On PONS, about US$20M of a US$30M headline day was manufactured, and "every $1 spent produces $282 of reported volume" ([ponsfiles](https://www.ponsfiles.com/)).
- StonkFun's buyback was overstated by 70% (US$2.09M claimed against US$1.23M). At least US$1.41M of reward money went to wallets tied to StonkFun but left out of its own ledger ([Bitquery](https://bitquery.io/investigations/is-stonkfun-dumping-on-holders)).
- Team wallets on the Solana AI project Kled sold about US$800k of tokens after promising a US$500k buyback ([report](https://pro-blockchain.com/en/solana-ai-project-kled-team-wallets-dumps-800k-tokens-after-promising-a-500k-buyback-plan)).
- Trench Bot's 6pm log classed 9 of 19 coins above US$500k as fake-market-cap farms.

## Evidence of money
- **Paying to be verified is a proven model next door.** TrustMRR (read-only Stripe-style keys, no crypto wallets supported per its [FAQ](https://trustmrr.com/faq)) made just over US$300k plus about US$30k in acquisition fees in its first 9 months, mostly from ad spots and marketplace fees ([Shipped & Paid](https://shippedandpaid.com/cases/trustmrr-verified-revenue-marketplace-330k-9-months-cursor/), self-reported). Its sidebar slots sold out at US$299, then US$699–1,499 a month ([Marc Lou](https://newsletter.marclou.com/p/i-made-an-app-in-24-hours-and-20-378-the-next-day)). **The on-chain sister niche has no equivalent.** TrustMRR doesn't read wallets, and Bags itself is listed on TrustMRR only through Stripe ([TrustMRR: BAGS](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bags)).
- **Coin teams already pay for legitimacy.** Every non-farm coin in Trench Bot's log paid the US$299 DexScreener Enhanced Token Info, and none of the 10 rugs or farms did. KIMCHIBULL paid US$3,999 for a 24-hour boost (Trench Bot, 18:10 scan).
- **Traders and agents pay per check.** Dexra charges 0.005 USDC per pre-trade check, and CrawlHive gates lookups by token holding (Git bot, 6pm radar). Cielo charges US$59–199 a month for alerts ([Cielo](https://cielo.finance/)). *Caution:* trader analytics subscriptions on TrustMRR are small (TraderSpy US$355 MRR from about 5,700 users ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/traderspy)); a Hyperliquid orderflow tool US$882 MRR ([category](https://trustmrr.com/category/crypto-web3))). So the plan leads with team-side revenue, not trader subscriptions.
- **The revenue pool being claimed is large.** Solana apps earned US$143M of protocol revenue in August 2026 ([Solana Compass / DefiLlama](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-tops-all-blockchains-for-app-revenue-in-august-2026-with-143m-capturing-38-of-global-total)). Bags reports US$26.16M of protocol revenue across 160.7k coins since January 2025 ([Bags stats](https://bags.holdings/revenue-stats/)).
- **Teammate signals (corroborated).** X bot: "verifiable usage" claims (TermiX) are "easy to screenshot and hard to trust" ([Armstro57](https://x.com/Armstro57/status/2113160596245946545)); "publish the dashboard and let usage speak." Trench Bot: farms dominate fake market cap, and traders need two-way, organic flows. Git bot: buy-side checks are turning into paid MCP, REST and Telegram tools (Dexra, CrawlHive), and its clone-family gap needs the same funder-clustering engine Revtrace uses.

**What survived all four checks.**
1. *Incumbents' own sites:* none separates independent payers from creator-funded ones.
2. *Platform directories:* pump.fun ships the deposit primitive but disclaims verification, and Pump Studio ranks by market cap. In MadeOnSol's Solana tool directory, ICM Analytics is the nearest listing, and it's curated and self-reported.
3. *GitHub and changelogs (last 30 days):* no repo for revenue verification, wash-revenue detection or proof of revenue on Solana (GitHub search returned 0). Only single-coin dashboards exist (brodie-terminal, hype-buyback-dashboard) and a pump.fun creator tracer CLI ([MarXol](https://github.com/idrode/MarXol)).
4. *Web and X by function:* revenue screeners exist (Gem Screener, ICM) but take revenue as given; the forensics are one-off.

**The gap:** a continuous, per-coin, long-tail organic-revenue check with team-signed fee wallets, a buyback check and alerts.

**Why incumbents won't build it.** DefiLlama is a neutral aggregator that lists protocols through adapters, and judging payers would mean accusing listed protocols. Launchpads (pump.fun, Bags, StonkFun) profit from volume and fees regardless of who pays, as PONS shows. Gem Screener and ICM chose the opposite approach: breadth from DefiLlama, or a curated list. Bitquery sells data and uses investigations as marketing.

## Product and user experience
**First minute.** Open revtrace (web) or the Telegram bot and paste a CA. You get a card like this:

`$VEIL · 7d revenue $61.4k → organic $X (N independent payers) · creator-linked $Y (Z%) · fees go to 2-of-2 team multisig, buyback claimed 20% → done W% · P/organic-rev 30d: Q×`

Each number links to its transactions on Solscan. One tap adds an alert (Pro). The leaderboard shows product coins by 7d and 30d organic revenue. Teams click "Verify my coin", sign with the creator wallet and fee wallets, pay US$149 in USDC or by card, and get a live badge to embed on their site.

**Under the hood (advanced but invisible).**
- **Revenue capture:** Tokenized Agent deposit addresses and invoice payments (`@pump-fun/agent-payments-sdk`, invoice PDAs); declared or team-signed fee wallets for other coins; x402 receipts.
- **Payer-independence graph:** for each paying wallet, walk funding back up to 3 hops (first funder, common funders, timing). Flag "creator-linked" when the funding reaches the creator, dev or insider cluster (creator from pump.fun's API, plus same-block or same-funder bundles). Mark CEX-funded payers "unattributed", never "organic". Detect circular flows (deposit → creator claim → new wallets → deposit). Thresholds follow the ponsfiles method: self-funded opening buy, 40+ wallets, funder share.
- **Buyback check:** compare the claimed % (pump.fun's on-chain buyback setting or the team's public claim) with actual on-chain buys and burns per week.
- **Price-to-organic-revenue** on circulating market cap (DexScreener/Birdeye price), shown as a fact, never as "upside".
- **Wording rule:** "not shown to be organic", not "fake". Methodology page, evidence links and a right-of-reply form on every flag.

**MVP stack:** Next.js on Vercel (free), Supabase (free), a TypeScript indexer on a small VPS with Helius webhooks and RPC (free 1M credits, US$49/month for 10M; [Helius](https://www.helius.dev/pricing)), grammY Telegram bot, Solana Pay plus Lemon Squeezy, x402 for the API.

**Week-by-week (solo, about 25–30 h/week).**
- **W1:** enumerate Tokenized Agents and deposits; seed 30 revenue coins' fee wallets from their own dashboards; funding-trace worker.
- **W2:** scoring (organic / creator-linked / unattributed), buyback check, coin page plus leaderboard.
- **W3:** Telegram `/rev <CA>`, verify-my-coin flow (wallet signatures plus payment), embeddable badge.
- **W4:** Pro alerts, x402/MCP endpoint, methodology page, Revenue Census #1 report (draft for Ethan).

**What's hard.** Getting false positives low (shared funders such as CEX hot wallets and faucet-style funders). Keeping credit use inside RPC limits during bursts. Covering fee designs that aren't simple transfers (tax tokens, LP fees). Legal-safe wording.

**What a copycat clones in a weekend:** a leaderboard of reported revenue (Gem Screener already is one). **What they can't clone quickly:** the payer-graph labels with a low error rate, the archive of team-signed fee wallets, and months of history.

## Token design
- **Jobs:** (1) **Access:** hold or stake a set amount of $TRACE for Pro alerts, as an alternative to US$19 a month. (2) **Spend:** deep traces (5-hop graph plus PDF evidence pack) cost $TRACE, and the tokens are burned. (3) **Flag bond:** anyone can stake $TRACE to flag a coin's revenue for review. If the flag is upheld the bond comes back, and a listed USDC bounty is paid **for the work** out of the operating budget. If it's rejected the bond is burned. (4) Teams can pay verification in $TRACE at a discount.
- **Launch route: Bags.** It links the mint to the creator's X account and has a public creator-fee pool; Tapeline's review compared Bags, pump.fun and Believe fees. Launch at a real event: publishing Revenue Census #1, which fits Trench Bot's "real trigger at minute zero" finding. Mint and freeze revoked, small labelled dev buy, no bundles.
- **On-chain fee flow and verification:** USDC verification and API income go to a public wallet; $TRACE creator fees go to the Bags creator wallet. Revtrace lists **itself** on its own leaderboard, with product revenue and creator fees reported separately, so its own token can't hide behind its own tool.
- **Creator fees vs subscriptions:** plan on **zero** token income. CrawlHive, a real product with users, sits at about US$20k market cap (Trench Bot). Subscriptions and verifications are the business.
- **Public repo from day one:** the indexer, scoring rules and methodology are open, so anyone can reproduce a label. The wallet-cluster label database and API keys stay private.
- **Comparables:** Believe Screener ($BSCREENER) is a tokenised screener. Bubblemaps (BMT) and Kaito (KAITO) are analytics tokens. X bot found $FRENS and Gomo terminal coins pushed by templated shilling; Trench Bot found that PANGEA's revenue buyback drew a "rigged" label. So: no paid callers, no buybacks.
- **Legal line:** no revenue share, no buybacks from revenue, no staking yield, no profit language. The flag bounty is payment for a service, not a holder return. Featured slots are labelled "sponsored" and never change a coin's numbers.

## Defensibility
- Team-signed fee-wallet registry and badge backlinks: network effects like TrustMRR's.
- A growing archive of labelled wallet clusters (creator, insider, farm funders), shared with Git bot's clone-family gap.
- A record as a neutral referee, built on public methodology and reproducible labels.
- Being first to cover Tokenized Agents. All of this is modest; the moat is data plus trust, built over months.

## Kill or confirm (30 days, under A$150 cash)
- **Spike (days 1–3, A$0):** count Tokenized Agents with deposits in the last 30 days, total deposits, and the organic share across 30 seeded revenue coins. **Kill** if total traceable revenue across all coins found is under US$50k per 30 days (too small to referee).
- **Accuracy (days 4–10):** hand-check 40 flagged payers. **Pass** if under 5% are wrong; **kill or rework** if over 10%.
- **Paid validation (days 11–30, needs Ethan's OK for outreach and posting):** publish Census #1 and offer founding verification at US$99 and Pro at US$19.
  - **Confirm** if: at least 8 paid verifications, at least 10 paid Pro users, and at least 150 bot users with at least 30% returning in week 2.
  - **Kill** if: fewer than 3 verifications **and** fewer than 5 Pro users.
- Cash: domain plus one month of VPS ≈ A$35; Helius stays on the free tier. The legal review comes only if the test confirms.

## Candidates checked this run and why they lost
- **Clone-family check for pre-trade APIs** (Git bot, Trench Bot): per-mint scanners are plentiful (Dexra, CrawlHive, RugCheck) and there's no proof anyone pays for clustering alone. Its funder-clustering engine is reused here instead.
- **Pre-IPO perps tracker:** [data.hyperliquid.build/pre-ipo](https://data.hyperliquid.build/pre-ipo/) and Talos already show implied valuations for free.
- **HIP-3 analytics:** HypeTrad, HIP3RLIQUID, HypurrView and a HIP-3 funding-comparison site, all free.
- **Key inventory after Railway** (X bot's top pain): still crowded (Doppler, Infisical, GitGuardian, as of 08:30 today).
- **More builder-code frontends:** fomo makes US$1.52M in 30 days, but pump.fun and wallets are moving in, and there's the AFSL risk (Tapeline lesson).
