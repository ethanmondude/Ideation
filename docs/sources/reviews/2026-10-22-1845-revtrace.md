## Verdict
- **Score: 5/10. Lead, not thesis (capped: no real proof anyone pays for a payer check). Token fit 5/10.** Ranked 5th of 7, level with Second Look, behind Ledgerport (6.5), Tapeline (6), Mint Seal and Breach Kit (5.5).
- **Biggest correction:** "no one checks whether the payers are independent" is wrong. The exact engine already exists for x402 and Base agent revenue: [Dewash](https://opensea.io/tools/x402-bazaar/base/297175214257758199) (US$4.99 per check, "two hop on-chain USDC funding trace separates organic demand from circular self payment rings", payable on Base or Solana), [x402watch](https://github.com/solana-foundation/pay-skills/pull/36) (labels every x402 buyer wallet organic / self-test / suspected wash), [x402stats](https://x402stats.io/methodology) (raw vs organic revenue), [ChainWard](https://chainward.ai/decodes/termix-on-chain) (free on-chain decodes) and [Commonworld Atlas](https://commonworld.one/atlas/) (claims vs evidence). Allium sells x402 tables with self-dealing flags. What's left is narrower: **Solana product-coin fee wallets and pump.fun Tokenized Agent deposits**, which none of these cover.
- **Biggest money gap:** the closest paid product has almost no buyers. x402gle shows Dewash at **1 settlement all time (US$4.99)** ([x402gle](https://x402gle.com/resources/75f5b522-b774-4d21-9d75-27e38eb4fd49)). ChainWard's checks are free. TrustMRR, the model the thesis copies, makes verification **free** and earns from sponsors and its marketplace ([FAQ](https://trustmrr.com/faq)). So the US$149 team verification fee is the untested core. Best path: free reports to build an audience first, then sell sponsor slots and token-gated alerts, with a small pre-sale to test whether teams pay.

- **Money already moving (close by):** DexScreener Enhanced Token Info at US$299 was paid by 6 coins in Trench Bot's log. That buys visibility, though, not scrutiny. - **Unit economics (estimates):** cost to serve is low (indexing only deposit and fee-wallet inflows, not every trade). The thesis's above-90% margin is plausible. CAC is about A$0 cash if reports spread. Payback is immediate on any up-front fee. The unknown is conversion, not cost.
- **Bottom-up size (estimate, thesis assumptions):** about US$7k–10k MRR ceiling on Solana even if teams pay. If teams won't pay, the model drops to sponsor slots plus Pro alerts, roughly the thesis's US$1.5k bear case. Treat US$10k/month as unlikely without Base and x402, where free rivals already exist.
- **Wedge and moat:** the wedge is a public Tokenized Agent organic-revenue leaderboard (a census). The moat is thin: a labelled funder-wallet archive, team-signed fee wallets and a record of accurate calls. ChainWard or x402stats could add Solana coins quickly.
- **Route to first US$10k/month without ads:** not credible on teams alone. A realistic order: (1) free census reports and coin pages to earn an audience; (2) sponsor slots, labelled "sponsored", at whatever traffic supports (TrustMRR now charges US$399 per 7 days with a 200k-visitor-a-month audience; expect far less); (3) Pro alerts paid in USDC or by holding $TRACE; (4) team verification as an upsell when a coin is flagged. All estimates.

## 6. Build and revenue plan
**Legal first (Ethan is in Perth).** Labelling a named coin's revenue "creator-linked" is a statement about the people behind it. In Australia:
- Individuals can sue for defamation, and so can companies with fewer than 10 employees. Companies must show serious financial loss ([Model Defamation Provisions](https://pcc.gov.au/uniform/2020/Consolidated_Model_Defamation_Provisions.pdf) s 9, s 10A).
- Larger companies can still sue for injurious falsehood, which needs malice or recklessness ([SLK Lawyers](https://www.slklawyers.com.au/can-corporations-sue-for-defamation-in-australia-why-the-serious-harm-threshold-often-fails-business-plaintiffs/)).
- I haven't checked whether WA's Act has the 2021 serious-harm and public-interest changes. Ask the lawyer.

So: spend the A$300 legal reserve **before** publishing labels on named coins, not after. Use the "not shown to be organic" wording, publish evidence links and give a right of reply. Until then, publish aggregate census stats with no coin names.
