# Review: Askwatch (see every public answer @grok gives about your product on X)

*Project Bot review · 22 Oct 2026, 8:45pm AWST (Perth time) · thesis: /workspace/idea-bot/theses/2026-10-22-2030-askwatch.md (idea bot, 8:30pm AWST, thesis 9) · lens: solo, bootstrapped, ~A$1,000 total, Ethan builds full-stack web apps, audience = crypto-native tech bros, own token required (pump.fun only) · room notes: inputs-2026-10-22-2030.md (8:33pm section) and inputs-2026-10-22-2015.md*

## Verdict
- **Score: 5/10. Lead, not thesis (capped: nobody yet pays for a feed of Grok's real public replies; the money next door is for synthetic prompts). Token fit 4/10.** Ranked 5th of 8, level with Second Look and Revtrace, behind Ledgerport (6.5), Tapeline (6), Mint Seal and Breach Kit (5.5).
- **Biggest correction: the feed is much thinner per brand than the thesis implies.** The 212k–244k a day total is right (I re-ran it). But the "123 to 189 a day for 10 crypto tools" is inflated by common words (Jupiter, Phantom, Photon, Axiom). The 6 unambiguous names (GMGN, BullX, DexScreener, Rugcheck, Bubblemaps, Helius) together got **29 Grok replies in 7 days**. pump.fun itself got **25 in 7 days**. TrustMRR got **5**. KIMCHI's contract address appeared in **1**. So a small brand or coin gets roughly zero to a few a week. Only big brands or people get a usable daily feed. The "tens a day for a mid-size brand" line doesn't hold up.
- **Biggest money gap and new risk:** "what Grok says about you" is already sold, by at least 8 synthetic-prompt trackers that include Grok (Trakkr from US$100, Ossvy Growth US$199, Brand24's Chatbeat add-on, Meev, Rankability, Livesov, AthenaHQ, and Licter as a consultancy). None of them indexes @grok's real thread replies, so the gap is real but feature-sized. **X's own Developer Policy may block it.** It says developers "should never use the X API to measure the availability, performance, functionality, or usage of X for benchmarking, competitive, or commercial purposes" ([policy](https://docs.x.com/developer-terms/policy)). Grok is part of X, so get that cleared in writing before building. Best path: a free "what Grok told people about you" lookup for handles and big brands, a paid tier aimed at crypto marketing agencies and KOLs (who can pool many thin feeds), and a counts-only test that costs cents.

## 3. Money check
- **Money already moving (close by):** AI-visibility tracking is proven paid demand. Rank Prompt has US$43.6k MRR from 213 subscriptions (about US$205 each), Elmo US$5.3k and Mentionable US$3.9k (TrustMRR, Stripe-verified), and Peec an estimated US$11.5M ARR (Sacra). Grok-specific tracking is sold at US$100 to US$199 a month (Trakkr, Ossvy). **For this exact job (real replies), money is zero.** There's no buyer, no quote and no pricing page anywhere. Crypto KOL and agency budgets exist (X bot keeps finding paid callers and template shill rings), but nobody has shown they'd spend them on reputation monitoring.
- **Unit economics (estimates, X's own prices):**
  - **Cost per tracked term:** one counts call (US$0.005) plus reading matched Grok replies and their parent questions (2 reads each, US$0.01 per answer). A brand with 20 Grok answers a week costs about US$0.90 a month. Even a 500-a-week agency client is about US$22 a month.
  - **Margins:** the thesis's 72–74% margin is conservative. On thin feeds, cost to serve is closer to 1–5% of a US$49 plan. Polling cost is fine if `since_id` stops dead queries from returning re-reads.
  - **The 3M-read cap** is about 150k answers a day across all customers. It's not a constraint until past US$10k MRR.
  - **CAC and payback:** CAC is about A$0 cash if reports spread, and payback is immediate. **The unknown is conversion and churn.** A thin feed means "nothing happened this week" emails, which kill retention.
- **Bottom-up size (estimates):** the thesis's 3,000 reachable products at 3–4% is too generous, because most small products get under one Grok answer a week. A better base:
  - Crypto marketing agencies and KOL managers (assume 200–400 reachable) at US$149–299.
  - Larger X-native devtools and Solana apps with 20+ Grok answers a week (assume 300–500) at US$49–129.
  - At 5% conversion that's about 25 to 45 accounts, or roughly **US$3k to US$7k MRR**.
  - **US$10k/month is possible only with an agency tier that works, or by bundling into a wider "Grok plus X reputation" product.**
- **Wedge and moat:** the wedge is a free instant lookup: "Grok answered 14 questions about @you in 7 days; 3 called you a shill; 1 repeated a wrong number." The moat is thin: fact-diff tuned to crypto (CA, official links, "unverified on-chain" claims), people and handle matching, and distribution. Since full-archive search is on pay-per-use, a rival can backfill history, so history isn't much of a moat either.
- **Route to first US$10k/month without ads (all estimates):** (1) a free handle and brand lookup page, shareable cards, and the `/grok @handle` Telegram command; (2) Pro at US$29–49 for KOLs, founders and coin devs (alerts when Grok answers about them, with a fact-diff); (3) an agency tier at US$199–299 for 10–25 handles with white-label weekly reports; (4) an API for listening tools to pull "Grok said" data, which turns the copy risk into a channel. Expect about US$3–7k MRR on this path. US$10k needs agencies to adopt it widely.

## 5. Token-launch check
- **Job:** (1) holding $ASKW unlocks Pro alerts for your own handle or ticker instead of US$29/month; (2) spending $ASKW pays for deep backfills (30 days of Grok answers for any term, now possible via full-archive search) and rival audits, and the tokens spent are burned. That's burn-on-use from tokens users bought, not revenue-funded. Card and USDC stay the main way to pay. **Plainly:** the core payers (agencies, founders) won't need the coin, so for them it's mostly speculation and a tip jar. The token-native variant that fits best: a **free Grok-mention watch on a coin's own ticker and team handles for teams holding a set amount**, posted in their Telegram. It's useful only once a coin gets talked about, though: KIMCHI's CA showed up in 1 Grok reply in 7 days. Fees are verifiable on-chain if USDC goes to a published wallet and burns come from a public app wallet.
- **Launch route: pump.fun (Ethan's rule; no meta shift per X bot and Trench Bot, 8pm–8:18pm).** Terms from [pump.fun's fees page](https://pump.fun/docs/fees) (last updated 17 Oct 2026, re-read 8:40pm AWST):
  - **Cost to create:** no platform fee, only the network fee and rent.
  - **Bonding curve:** 1.25% a trade, of which 0.30% goes to the creator.
  - **Graduation:** automatic, moves the coin to a canonical PumpSwap pool, 0.015 SOL.
  - **PumpSwap creator fee:** 0.30% under 420 SOL market cap, 0.95% at 420–1,470 SOL, then stepping down.
  - **Platform Fee:** since 17 Oct, trades through pump.fun's web and mobile apps also pay a Platform Fee that doesn't go to the creator.
  - **Holder Rewards:** these would send the creator fee to holders (minimum US$20 holding). That's a holder payout, so it stays **off**.
  - **Tokenized Agent:** stays **off**. It runs hourly buyback-and-burn from deposits, a revenue-funded buyback.
  - **Fee sharing:** creator fees can be split across wallets ([fee sharing](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/main/docs/instructions/CREATOR_FEE_SHARING.md)).
- **Creator fees vs subscriptions (estimate, not a forecast):** at US$5k a day of volume, 0.30% is about US$15 a day (~US$450 a month), or about US$48 a day in the 0.95% band. Tool tokens without usage fade: $AGENCY is at about US$3.4k market cap and US$43 of 24h volume (Git bot), and HeyGrok "fell" (tracker). Plan on zero. Subscriptions are the business.
- **Evidence from comparable tokens:** Larpcheckbot's $LARPCHECK is the nearest reply-bot token, and its job is unverified (X bot). X bot found none of 15 checked pump.fun contracts organic, so the token can't lean on callers. Trench Bot found that clean coins launched on a real event with a named person, and every brand-name coin today (Grok, Gemini, RockstarGames) rugged or is set up to.
- **Risks:**
  - **ASIC:** no revenue share, buybacks or holder rewards, and no profit language.
  - **Name:** don't use "Grok" in the token's name, ticker or the product name. That's a trademark problem, and it matches today's name-bait rug template.
  - **Customer fear:** agencies and devtool buyers may read a coin as a casino side-project, so keep the token off the main pricing page.
  - **Conflict of interest:** a tool reporting what Grok says about coins shouldn't rank coins.
- **Token fit: 4/10. Plan:** $ASKW on pump.fun (SOL pair; Holder Rewards and Tokenized Agent off; mint and freeze revoked; no bundles; small labelled dev buy), used to unlock own-handle and ticker alerts while held and burned on deep backfills. Launch on the day the first public "Grok said what?" report goes out, with the CA posted from Askwatch's official X account, **only after 10+ paying accounts, a written OK from X on the API use case, and a token-wording check.**
