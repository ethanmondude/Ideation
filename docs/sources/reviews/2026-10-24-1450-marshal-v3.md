### Claude Code build brief (v3 Marshal, updated at this re-score)
*Build Marshal v1 from this section. Base documents, for detail only: idea bot's v3 thesis §"Build brief for Claude Code" (`/workspace/idea-bot/theses/2026-10-24-1430-marshal-v3.md`, sha256 4a5d4610…) and the v2 brief earlier in this file (AT-01–AT-31, TB-1..15, data model, legal wording). **Summary.** Marshal is a read-only Chrome MV3 extension (Brave, Arc and Edge work; Firefox later) for desktop Solana traders who find coins on X and buy on GMGN, Jupiter, pump.fun, Axiom or Photon. On x.com it folds drainer, airdrop-lure and copy-paste shill replies into one grey bar with a reason, puts a factual pill on every CA, $ticker and token link, flags a reply CA that differs from the thread author's, shows "claimed $X · live $Y" when a post brags about market cap, marks a coin pushed by a shill ring it has seen before, and decodes QR codes in post images. When you click through to a coin page, it remembers the post, and if the page's mint differs it shows: "⚠ Not the mint in the post you came from (@author, 3 min ago)". No wallet access, no buy button, no sign-up, no LLM, and post text and images never leave the browser.

**One-minute wow.** Install. Open a memecoin reply thread: nine lure replies are folded, one reply's CA has a red pill ("different CA from the one @author posted"). Click a reply's chart link to GMGN: a red banner at the top says it isn't the mint from the post you came from. Nothing to set up.

**Target user.** Tech-bro desktop traders (Chrome, Brave, Arc) who trade off crypto Twitter. They pay by card, USDC or by holding $MRSHL after launch. Mobile is out (the X app takes no extensions).

#### Data model
- **Extension IndexedDB:** `verdicts{mint, family_count, oldest, deepest, deployer_launches, live_mc, fetched_at}`; `accounts{handle, posts_seen, ca_posts, bio_hash, first_seen, last_seen}`; `edges{a, b, ca, window_start}`; `rings{id, members[], cas[], coin_record{down90, holding}, updated_at}`.
- **`chrome.storage.session`:** `clicks[{mint, post_id, author, ts}]` (newest 20, 30-min TTL).
- **Server (Upstash):** `mint:{mint}` cached facts (TTL 60 s for MC, 24 h for deployer), `spend:{yyyymm}` counter. No handles, no post text.
