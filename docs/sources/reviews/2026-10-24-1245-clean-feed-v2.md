**Summary.** Clean Feed is a read-only Chrome extension (Firefox build as a stretch) for tech-bro Solana traders on desktop x.com. It folds drainer, airdrop-lure, caller-ad and copy-paste shill replies into one grey bar with a crypto-specific reason. It also puts a factual pill on every CA, $ticker and token link in posts and replies. The pill uses the thread itself as evidence: "different CA from the one @author posted in this thread", "1 of 12+ mints called $KIMCHI", "oldest ≠ deepest", "deployer 825 launches". It never names "the real one" until the engine passes Mint Seal Part B (the flag `realCall` stays off). There's no wallet in the extension, no buy button, no sign-up and no LLM, and post text never leaves the browser. Revenue comes from Pro (US$9/month or holding $CFEED) and agents (MCP plus x402 at US$0.005 a check).

**One-minute wow.** Install, then open any memecoin reply thread on x.com. Nine drainer and shill replies are already folded ("Hidden · 10%-supply airdrop lure · same text as 9 others · Show"). The one reply pushing a different contract has a red pill: "different CA from the one @kimchi1x posted in this thread". Every other CA carries a grey line saying how many mints share its ticker. There's nothing to set up.

**Target user.** Desktop Chrome, Brave or Arc users who find coins on crypto Twitter and in X Communities and trade on Axiom, GMGN or Photon. Community mods and caller-group admins are a secondary group. They pay with a card, USDC or by holding $CFEED. Mobile is out, because the X app can't run extensions.

#### Data model
- **Extension (IndexedDB, never uploaded):**
  - `verdicts[mint]`: pill JSON, `checked_at`. TTL 10 minutes for mints under 24h old, 6h for older ones.
  - `threads[conversation_id]`: root author, root CAs by $T.
  - `seen[author]`: CAs and tickers by conversation, kept 60 minutes.
  - `dupkeys[key]`: count and authors, kept 24h.
  - `accounts[handle]`: always-show or always-hide.
  - `settings`: strictness, raw, local-only.
  - `rulepack_version`.
  - `free_counter[date]`.
  - `pro_token`.
- **Server (Upstash, one hash per key with a TTL):**
  - `mint:{mint}`: symbol, name, created_slot, mint_auth, freeze_auth, graduated, reserves_quote (on-chain), holders, deployer, first10s_buyers, family_key, flags[], shadow_candidate, checked_at.
  - `family:{norm_name}`: mints[], truncated (bool), fetched_at. TTL 10 minutes.
  - `deployer:{addr}`: launches, graduations, last_launch. TTL 7 days.
  - `link:{etld1}`: rule, list_hit.
- **Counters (Workers Cache API, not Redis):**
  - `spend:{yyyymm}`
  - `rl:{install_id}:{date}`
- **Shadow log** (D1 table `shadow(mint, family_key, candidate, votes_json, ts)`, within the D1 free write limit) for the Part B prediction file.
- **Pro (after launch):**
  - `pro:{install_id}`: plan (card or hold), wallet (if held), expires.
  - Hold re-checked every 30 days through Helius `getTokenAccountsByOwner`.

#### Token integration ($CFEED on pump.fun, only after the launch gate and Ethan's pick)
- **What's on-chain:**
  - one SPL token created on pump.fun, with mint and freeze authority confirmed off;
  - a labelled dev buy of no more than 0.5 SOL from a published wallet;
  - creator fees paid to a published operating wallet used for hosting only.
  
  Nothing else runs on-chain in v1: no program, no staking, no escrow.
- **v1 mechanic: hold to unlock Pro.**
  - The website asks for a `signMessage` nonce (never a transaction).
  - The server reads the SPL balance. At or above the threshold, it issues a 30-day Pro token to the install ID.
- **v1.1 mechanic: burn your own** for deep checks, priced under Rempart's US$0.15.
  - The user signs an SPL `burn` of their own tokens.
  - The server confirms mint, amount and owner on-chain, then credits the account.
  - The public ledger links each credit to its burn signature.
- **Never:** holder payouts, buybacks, revenue share, staking yield, creator-fee buy-and-burn, Holder Rewards or Tokenized Agent. Card and USDC revenue never buys $CFEED.
- **pump.fun fees** ([fees page](https://pump.fun/docs/fees), updated 17 Oct 2026, re-read 12:32pm AWST):
  - Creating a coin carries no platform fee.
  - Bonding-curve trades pay 1.25%, of which 0.30% goes to the creator.
  - Graduation costs 0.015 SOL.
  - After graduation, the canonical PumpSwap pool pays the creator 0.95% at a market cap of 420–1,470 SOL, falling to 0.05% above 98,240 SOL.
  - Web and mobile app trades add a 0.45% Platform Fee, including the creator's first buy.
- **Dogfood:** add $CFEED to the extension's family map at launch, so its copycats get stamped from minute one.

#### Legal wording guardrails (ASIC; not legal advice; the A$300 consult (est.) signs off before launch)
- [INFO 225](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/digital-transformation/digital-assets-financial-products-and-services): a label like "utility" doesn't decide a token's status; its rights and benefits do. Example 8, a membership NFT that unlocks access, is "unlikely to be a managed investment scheme".
- Per Project Bot's 11:45am brief (not re-read this run): ASIC's no-action period ended 30 Sep 2026, and the DAF Act starts 9 Apr 2027.
- **Say:** "$CFEED unlocks Pro access in Clean Feed. It isn't an investment and gives no right to profits or revenue."
- **Never say:** returns, price targets, "number go up", "burns raise the price", "not a financial product", "ASIC-compliant" or "approved".
- No paid promoters ([INFO 269](https://www.asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/financial-services/giving-financial-product-advice/discussing-financial-products-and-services-online)).
- No token talk on the CWS listing.
- Pills state facts ("different CA from the one @A posted"), never "scammer" about a named person.

#### Launch checklist
1. **Pick a name.** Run a CWS, X and DexScreener check on "Mintmark", "CA Lens" and "Trench Filter" (none cleared yet).
2. **Ship the build:** register the domain, deploy the Worker, set the spend cap, then publish an unlisted CWS build with the privacy disclosure (single purpose, Limited Use, no remote code).
3. **Acceptance:** all Locked ATs pass. AT-16 has been run once (whichever way it went) and `realCall` is set from the result.
4. **Money test,** only once Ethan picks this thesis: a 30-day US$9 founding Pro. Pass is ≥25 paid and ≥30% of installs active 3+ days a week.
5. **Token,** only after the gate and the legal consult:
   - launch on pump.fun with Holder Rewards and Tokenized Agent off;
   - confirm the authorities are off on-chain;
   - make the labelled dev buy of ≤0.5 SOL and publish the no-sell policy (est. 90 days);
   - pin one CA on the site, the X account and the extension settings;
   - no bundles, snipers or paid callers.
6. **Ongoing:** a weekly pattern-pack refresh (data only) and a weekly rival changelog watch (EDGERUN, Buddy, Verixy, fable).
