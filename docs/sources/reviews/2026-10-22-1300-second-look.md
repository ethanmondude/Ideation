# Review: Second Look (pre-refund photo and claim check for Shopify brands on Gorgias)

*Project Bot review · 22 Oct 2026, 1:00pm AWST (Perth time) · thesis: /workspace/idea-bot/theses/2026-10-22-1230-second-look.md (idea bot, 12:30pm AWST) · lens: solo, bootstrapped, Ethan builds full-stack web apps · room notes: /workspace/project-bot/reviews/inputs-2026-10-22-1215.md (general bar only; none specific to this thesis), plus X bot and Git bot notes sent at 12:44pm AWST*

## Verdict
- **Score: 5/10. Lead, not thesis.** The pain is real and the buyers (Shopify brands) already pay for apps by card, but the gap the thesis claims is already filled inside Gorgias. Nobody has shown that small brands pay by card for photo checks on their own.
- **Biggest correction:** the thesis says it "found none" that sits at the refund decision inside Gorgias, uses Shopify history and back-scans past tickets. **Two products in the Gorgias App Store already do this.** [Backwise](https://www.gorgias.com/apps/backwise) (listed 5 Oct 2026) scores each refund request in the ticket sidebar using "the whole conversation and its photos", plus order, delivery and customer history, and runs a "Fraud Discovery Program" that analyses a merchant's past months of claims ([backwise.co](https://www.backwise.co/)). [Wyllo](https://wyllo.ai/wyllo-gorgias-partnership/) (NoFraud + Yofi) feeds risk scores "directly into the Gorgias AI Agent" so automated refunds can take risk into account. Verilo also posts its results into Gorgias tickets, and [Claimscan](https://claimscan.io/en) already offers a free audit of 25 to 50 past claim photos. So the hook and the moat the thesis relies on are both taken.
- **What's left:** a cheap, card-paying version ($39 to $99) for small Gorgias brands that sit below Backwise's floor ("1,000+ refund or return claims per month") and Wyllo's mid-market sales motion. That's also where the ROI is thinnest: the thesis's own maths puts about $200 a month at risk for a $5M-GMV brand.
- **Money gap:** money clearly moves at the mid-market level (Wyllo customers include Everlane and HexClad; Yofi was acquired). At the self-serve level there's no proof yet. Verilo has 0 reviews, PixelProof has 1 review after 5 months, ReturnsIQ has 0, Sentinel has fewer than 5 installs, and Backwise's Shopify listing has 0.

## 1. Fact-check

**Verified**
- **Gorgias AI Agent Vision.** The quote is exact: "If the image clearly shows a damaged item and the shopper is requesting help, offer to send a replacement or issue a refund based on our refund policy", with "act according to your policy without escalating to a human". Vision is on automatically and reads images with an LLM. Nothing on the page says it checks whether an image is real ([Gorgias docs](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/how-ai-agent-uses-vision-to-understand-images-1992836), updated about a month ago).
- **Gorgias custom HTTP Actions.** Correct. The advanced editor supports HTTP requests (GET/POST/PUT/DELETE/PATCH), Shopify customer and order variables, JSONPath output variables and conditional branches. **Two caveats the thesis missed:** the page says you "must have an active Automate subscription" and Lead/Admin rights to build one (the page is 3 months old, and the pricing page now says AI Agent is on every plan, so check this in a trial). Also, customer confirmation is a per-Action option ("You can also decide whether to require customer confirmation"). It isn't a built-in guardrail on irreversible actions as the thesis says ([Gorgias docs](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/create-actions-use-advanced-options-to-build-custom-actions-for-ai-agent-1060364)).
- **Gorgias AI pricing:** $0.90 per automated interaction on annual plans and $1.00 on monthly plans. If a human replies within 72 hours, the interaction is billed only as a ticket, which supports the thesis's "soft reason" that Gorgias earns less when tickets go back to humans ([Gorgias pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing)).
- **Ravelin:** 65% of consumers say AI has made false refund claims easier, from a survey of 6,200 shoppers in the State of Refunds 2026 report. Posted 19 January 2026 ([Ravelin](https://www.ravelin.com/blog/ai-powered-refund-abuse-dispute-fraud)). Ravelin's own advice is "assess the customer, not just the claim", which is the job Wyllo and Backwise already sell.
- **Modern Retail:** every quote checks out. Boll & Branch (Tannen: "so blatantly AI", $489 duvet sets, FaceTime check, a LinkedIn post with 2,000 likes, "there's probably some that got through"). Bogg ("a couple of times a month", "it ruins it for everybody else"). Yofi ("exploded overnight", "onesie-twosie to … daily"). Signifyd ("In another six months…"). Also NRF/Happy Returns (14%, $100B+) and eMarketer ($379B). **Context the thesis left out:** the same article says AI images are "still not the most popular fraud technique" and Bogg calls it "not all that frequent". The article is from early-to-mid 2026, going by its image path, so it isn't this week's news ([Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/technology/from-boll-branch-to-bogg-brands-are-battling-a-surge-of-ai-driven-return-fraud/)).
- **Verilo:** $29 (50 verifications) / $79 / $199, billed through Shopify. Results go into Gorgias and Zendesk tickets, and links can be sent "automatically through rules that match certain tickets". It's on Shopify from 14 Oct 2026, the press release came out 19 Oct, and it has 0 reviews ([The Paypers](https://thepaypers.com/fraud-and-fincrime/news/verilo-launches-live-verification-for-ecommerce-damage-claims), [pickyourapp](https://pickyourapp.com/products/verilo)).
- **PixelProof:** from $19 (30 claims). There are also $49, $199 and $799 tiers. Live camera only, launched 21 May 2026, 1 review ([Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/reklamation)).
- **Claimscan:** free for 10 a month, then €89 Growth, and "average fake claim: €180" (vendor figure). It also offers **a free forensic report on your last 25 to 50 claim photos within 48 hours**, which is the thesis's "backfill audit" hook ([Claimscan](https://claimscan.io/en)).
- **Kernvel ReturnsIQ:** $17 for 100 returns with photo authenticity checks. Launched 9 June 2026, 0 reviews ([Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/kernvel-returnsiq)).
- **Yofi:** "a 25% fee if we successfully prevent fraudulent returns that you deny". Works with Gorgias, Loop and Zendesk. 3 reviews ([Shopify App Store](https://apps.shopify.com/botnot-public-staging)). The NoFraud to Wyllo rebrand on 19 March 2026 is confirmed ([Wyllo](https://wyllo.ai/nofraud-rebrands-as-wyllo-introducing-a-cx-first-approach-to-ecommerce-risk-intelligence/)).
- **Truvane:** connectors for Shopify, Zendesk, Gorgias and others are "coming soon". It reports 98.5% recall, under 1% false positives and under 400ms (self-reported). **Note:** its own FAQ also says it "sits at the CS layer, in Zendesk, Gorgias, or Intercom", which contradicts the "coming soon" list ([Truvane](https://truvane.ai/)).
- **Sentinel (Zendesk):** free for 5 scans, "Less than 5" installs, no ratings ([Zendesk Marketplace](https://www.zendesk.com/marketplace/apps/support/1218601/sentinel/)).
- **Sightengine:** Starter is $29 a month for 10,000 operations, then $0.002 per operation. The AI-generated image model ("genai") costs **5 operations per image** ([pricing](https://sightengine.com/pricing), [operations FAQ](https://sightengine.com/faq/what-is-an-operation)). That works out to about $0.0145 per image within the quota and $0.01 per image on overage, so the thesis's figure is right.
- **Shopify revenue share:** 0% on the first US$1M of lifetime revenue, then 15%. **Missed by the thesis:** a 2.9% processing fee on all billing and a one-time US$19 App Store registration ([Shopify dev docs](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/distribution/revenue-share)).
- **HN thread:** "Using AI generated images to get refunds", 80 points and 100 comments, posted 31 Dec 2025, 10:23am AWST ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46440637)).

**Wrong, unsupported or out of date**
- **"None plugs into the AI agent's refund decision inside Gorgias…"** Wrong. Wyllo's signals are "ingestible directly into the Gorgias AI Agent" and can trigger macros and rules (live since July 2026 for joint customers) ([Wyllo + Gorgias](https://wyllo.ai/wyllo-gorgias-partnership/)). Backwise does refund-claim scoring in the Gorgias sidebar, takes photos into account, and returns a recommended action plus a verdict tag that rules can act on ([Gorgias App Store](https://www.gorgias.com/apps/backwise)).
- **"Backfill audit… the conversion moment the live-capture apps can't offer."** Claimscan (free audit of 25 to 50 past photos) and Backwise (analysis of past months of claims through Gorgias or Zendesk; [LinkedIn launch post](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/maximilien-guillot-55717284_backwise-est-en-ligne-il-y-a-deux-semaines-activity-7480655393304555520-QlXO)) already use it.
- **Missed competitors:** [HumanMeter Claims](https://humanmeter.app/claims/) (live-capture link that can be created "with one API call from your helpdesk"), [Securify](https://www.gorgias.com/apps/securify) (free Gorgias sidebar fraud score, listed 5 Oct 2026), [Pinch AI](https://www.gorgias.com/apps/pinch-ai) (returns-abuse scoring inside Gorgias) and [Extend](https://docs.gorgias.com/en-US/extend-1186284) (claim fraud flags in Gorgias). Also eDesk's photo checker already **halts its AI Agent on refund tickets** when a photo fails, which is the exact Second Look behaviour, built by a helpdesk itself ([eDesk](https://support.edesk.com/how-edesk-ai-protects-your-business-against-refund-fraud)).
- **Forensics on photos already received is the weak link.** HumanMeter says that in its own test, AI fakes that were re-saved "got past our detector 8 times out of 10" (self-reported). That's why Verilo, PixelProof and HumanMeter all moved to live capture. Second Look leads with passive forensics.
- **"About 2,500 Gorgias brands with AI Agent on."** The input is too high. Gorgias publishes "17,000+ ecommerce brands" ([homepage](https://www.gorgias.com/)) and "12,400+ brands" on Pro ([pricing](https://www.gorgias.com/pricing)), but no AI Agent count. A third-party crawl detected **2,176 companies** using Gorgias AI Agent on chat, with 435 churned, as of 18 July 2026 ([Bloomberry](http://bloomberry.com/data/gorgias-ai/), secondary, chat only, so a lower bound). The thesis assumed 3,750 to 6,000. See the money check.
- **"Wyllo from $250 a month."** Not found on Wyllo's site. Still unverified.
- **"Irreversible actions already require customer confirmation."** It's an option per Action, not a default (see above).

## 3. Money check
- **Money already moving:** Yes, at mid-market level, on contingency or by quote (Wyllo/Yofi, Backwise POC fees, Ravelin, Signifyd). Self-serve photo-check apps: **no proof of paying users** (0 to 1 reviews across Verilo, PixelProof, ReturnsIQ and Backwise on Shopify; fewer than 5 Sentinel installs). Category prices are listed from $17 to $199, but a listed price isn't proof that anyone pays it.
- **Unit economics (thesis's estimates, checked):** Sightengine genai costs about $0.01 to $0.0145 per photo. At about 1.5 photos per claim plus about $0.005 for the vision-LLM check and storage, that's **about $0.03 per check (estimate)**. Growth at $99 for 600 checks costs about $18 to serve, minus Shopify's 2.9% fee (about $3), leaving **about 79% gross margin at full use**. Running cost isn't the problem. If accuracy forces a switch to Truvane or Hive, those prices aren't public.
- **Customer ROI:** the thesis's own example (a $5M-GMV brand at $100 AOV, with 1% of orders becoming claims and 5% of those fake) puts **about $200 a month at risk (estimate)**. A $99 plan pays back only if it catches about 1 fake a month. Bogg sees "a couple of times a month". That's thin and leads to churn when it "never catches anything".
- **Market size, bottom-up (revised estimate):** 2,176 brands detected with AI Agent on chat (lower bound) to about 5,000 (the thesis's upper assumption), times about 50% that sell physical goods with photo refunds, gives **about 1,100 to 2,500 brands**. 100 paying customers would be 4% to 9% of that, competing with free Securify, Backwise, Wyllo and Verilo in the same App Store. Revenue ceiling: about $1.3M to $3.0M ARR at $99 (estimate).
- **Wedge and moat:** the only wedge left is price and self-serve for brands below about 500 to 1,000 claims a month. There's no technology moat (detection is bought in and others say it's beaten by re-saving). A cross-store photo-hash list would only become a moat at a few hundred stores.
- **Route to $10k MRR:** 100 × $99 means about 8 net new paying brands a month from month 3. That assumes App Store pull in a fraud category where the newest apps have 0 to 1 reviews. **Unproven, so this is a lead.**

## 5. Build plan (solo, bootstrapped)

**Don't build the 4-week app yet.** Spend 2 weeks on a money test that answers one question: will small Gorgias brands (under about 500 claims a month) pay $49 or more by card for this, knowing Backwise, Wyllo and Verilo exist?

**Stack:** Shopify app template (React Router/Remix, Polaris) or Next.js; Postgres (Neon free tier); Cloudflare R2 for short-lived images; Sightengine genai, behind a swappable provider interface; open-source pHash plus EXIF/C2PA parsing; Stripe Payment Links for the test; hosting on Fly.io or Render.

**Features, in order**
1. **Days 1 to 4:** a free web claim-photo checker (upload, then AI-image score, EXIF/C2PA, and pHash reuse against the store's own product images), giving a one-page report.
2. **Days 3 to 6:** a copy-paste Gorgias HTTP Action template ("Verify damage claim" returns `{decision, reasons[], stepup_url}`), tested in a Gorgias trial. Check first whether Actions still need Automate.
3. **Days 5 to 10:** a back-scan script. Gorgias REST API for tickets tagged damaged or defective in the last 90 days, plus Shopify refund and claim history per customer, giving a PDF audit with $ at risk.
4. **Only if the gate passes (weeks 3 to 4):** a live-capture step-up page, then the Shopify embedded app with Billing, then Gorgias App Store submission.

**Monthly running cost (estimate):** about A$50 to A$120 during the test (Sightengine US$29 or the free tier, hosting US$0 to 25, domain), plus one-off costs of US$19 for Shopify registration and about A$500 for a terms-of-service review if it's built. About US$100 to 300 a month at 50 customers (the thesis's estimate).

**First 10 paying users**
- 40 Shopify brands on Gorgias in breakables, linens, homewares and bulky goods, with $60+ AOV and no-return refunds, that are too small for Backwise. Find them through Gorgias case studies and public Gorgias chat widgets.
- 2 or 3 Gorgias partner agencies that set up AI Agent for clients (template plus a referral fee).
- One public write-up: "Gorgias's own Vision guidance refunds from a photo. Here's a fake getting through, and the 10-minute fix."
- **All outreach and posting needs Ethan's OK and goes out from his accounts.**

**30-day money test (thresholds are our estimates)**
- Offer a free 90-day back-scan to 30 CX leads. Aim for 12 completed scans, then pitch a $49-a-month founding plan by Stripe link.
- Ask every prospect what they use today (Wyllo, Backwise, Verilo, Claimscan, nothing).
- **Confirm (build weeks 3 to 4)** if 5 or more pay $49 or more by card **and** the merchants agree there are 3 or more likely fakes across the scans.
- **Kill** if 1 or fewer pay, **or** there's less than 1 likely fake per 100 claims, **or** a third or more of prospects already use a rival, **or** more than 8% of photos the merchant confirms are real get flagged.
- **Pivot** if fakes show up but nobody pays monthly: sell a $199 one-off audit. Claimscan does it free, so price on including Shopify history.
